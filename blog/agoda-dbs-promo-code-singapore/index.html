How to Apply Your Agoda DBS Promo Code Singapore Step by Step

For the Evergreen 18% Off Deal (any day):

  1. Go to www.agoda.com/dbs - not the main Agoda homepage.
  2. Search for your hotel in Singapore (or your destination) with your dates.
  3. Look for the "Coupon applicable" tag on the hotel and room listing - only tagged rooms qualify.
  4. Select a prepaid room type (not "pay at hotel" - those do not qualify).
  5. At checkout, enter your DBS or POSB card details. The 18% discount is applied automatically - no code entry needed.

For the Shiok Sundays 50% Off Deal (Sundays only):

  1. On a Sunday at or after 1AM SGT, visit www.agoda.com/dbssunday.
  2. Note the weekly promo code displayed in the header of the page - it refreshes each Sunday and runs on a fastest-fingers-first basis.
  3. Search for your hotel and select a "Coupon applicable" prepaid room.
  4. Enter the weekly code at checkout and pay with a DBS or POSB card.
  5. The 50% discount (capped at SGD 100, min. spend SGD 200) is applied before payment is confirmed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use the Agoda DBS promo for local Singapore hotels, not just overseas stays?
Yes, with a caveat. The evergreen deal at agoda.com/dbs applies to selected hotels worldwide, including properties in Singapore. You just need to check that your chosen room shows the "Coupon applicable" tag - if it does, the DBS discount applies regardless of whether the hotel is in Singapore or abroad.

Is the Agoda DBS promotion stackable with the Agoda Welcome Discount or other promo codes?
No. The DBS promotion cannot be combined with other discount codes, promotions, cashback portals, loyalty programs, or other Agoda codes. The one allowed stack is Agoda PointsMax - you can still earn frequent flyer miles through PointsMax on top of the DBS bank discount.

Which DBS card gives the best value on Agoda?
All DBS and POSB cards give the same Agoda discount percentage. The difference is the miles or cashback earned on top of the hotel saving. The DBS Woman's World Mastercard earns 4 miles per SGD 1 on online spend - one of the stronger options for frequent Agoda bookers who collect miles.

Do I need to register my DBS card on Agoda before using the promo?
No pre-registration is required. Book through the dedicated DBS link, select your hotel, and enter your DBS/POSB card at checkout. The discount is applied automatically once the system confirms a valid DBS/POSB card.

What is the maximum savings per Agoda DBS booking in Singapore?
The maximum is SGD 100 per booking under the Shiok Sundays 50% deal, which requires a minimum spend of SGD 200 and must be booked on a Sunday. The evergreen 18% deal is capped at SGD 30 per booking. Both caps are per booking, not per night.

Can foreigners with DBS cards use the Agoda DBS promo for Singapore hotels?
Yes. The promotion is open to any cardholder holding a valid DBS or POSB card with the DBS Bank name or logo on the card face, regardless of nationality. DBS accounts held by foreigners residing in Singapore qualify as long as the card is valid and in good standing.

What happens if the Shiok Sundays promo code shows "Invalid code" at checkout?
It means the weekly allocation has been fully redeemed. Codes are first-come, first-served from 1AM SGT each Sunday. If you miss out, use the evergreen deal at agoda.com/dbs for 18% off - no code needed and no weekly quota to compete for.
