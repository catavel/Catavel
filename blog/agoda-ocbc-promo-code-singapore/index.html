How to Book Agoda With Your OCBC Card, Step by Step

When the OCBC-Agoda bilateral promotion is active:

  1. Go directly to www.agoda.com/ocbc - not the main Agoda homepage.
  2. Search for your hotel and dates. Look for the "Coupon Applicable" tag on the room listing - only tagged rooms receive the OCBC discount.
  3. Select a prepaid room type (not "pay at hotel" - those do not qualify).
  4. At checkout, enter your OCBC credit or debit card. The discount is applied automatically - no code entry needed.
  5. Confirm and pay. The discounted amount is shown on the payment confirmation page.

Using the Mastercard-Agoda promotion (available now):

  1. Check the Mastercard promotions page or agoda.com for the current Mastercard-Agoda dedicated link.
  2. Access the booking page via that link - your Mastercard tier determines the discount level.
  3. Search for your hotel, select a "Coupon Applicable" prepaid room.
  4. Pay with your OCBC Mastercard (90°N MC, Frank, Voyage, etc) at checkout.
  5. Discount is applied at payment confirmation - 8% for standard Mastercard, 12% for World, 16% for World Elite.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there an active agoda ocbc promo code singapore for 2026?
There is no dedicated OCBC-Agoda bilateral discount running as of June 2026. The last confirmed campaigns expired in March 2025 (18% off, capped SGD 40) and September 2024 (7% off, no cap). OCBC Mastercard holders can access the Agoda-Mastercard network promotion (8-16% off depending on card tier), and universal Agoda codes like AGODATPT work with any OCBC card.

Does agoda.com/ocbc still work?
The agoda.com/ocbc page exists as a dedicated landing page for the OCBC partnership. When a bilateral promotion is active, it shows discounted rates with the "Coupon Applicable" tag. Between campaigns, it may show standard Agoda rates with no additional discount. Check the page directly - if rooms show a coupon discount, the promotion is live.

Which OCBC card gives the best value on Agoda?
For instant discounts, the OCBC Voyage (Mastercard World Elite) gives the highest Mastercard-Agoda promo tier at 16% off (capped SGD 60). For miles earning, OCBC 90°N Mastercard and Visa both earn 6-7 miles per SGD 1 on Agoda spend, making them the best choice for frequent Agoda bookers who collect miles.

Can OCBC debit cards use the Agoda OCBC promo?
Yes, when the OCBC-Agoda bilateral promotion is active. Past campaign T&Cs confirmed that any card bearing the OCBC name or logo qualifies, including debit cards. For the Mastercard-Agoda network promo, your OCBC debit card must be on the Mastercard network and at the qualifying tier.

Can I stack an Agoda promo code with the OCBC Mastercard promotion?
Agoda's bank card promotions are non-cumulative - they cannot be combined with other Agoda discount codes or promotions. However, you can earn OCBC card miles and Agoda PointsMax miles on top of a bank card discount, as loyalty mile earning is treated separately from the hotel price discount.

When will the next OCBC-Agoda promotion launch?
The OCBC-Agoda bilateral campaign has historically renewed roughly every quarter, though there is no fixed schedule. Monitor the OCBC Promotions page and agoda.com/ocbc for the next campaign. When a new campaign goes live, OCBC typically announces it via their promotions page and email newsletter to cardholders.

Are there other Agoda deals OCBC cardholders can access besides the bilateral promo?
Yes - three options: the Agoda-Mastercard network promo (for OCBC Mastercards, 8-16% off), the Agoda-Visa network promo (for OCBC Visa cards, 7% off via dedicated link), and universal Agoda codes from the Agoda Deals page like AGODATPT (10% off up to USD 100, any card). These are all available independently of any OCBC bilateral campaign.
