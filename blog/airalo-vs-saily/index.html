Airalo vs Saily in 2026, Best Travel eSIM

By Catavel Published 2026-05-17 Updated 2026-05-21

Choosing between Airalo vs Saily for your travel eSIM? Both are excellent options, but they suit slightly different travelers. Airalo is the pioneer - wider coverage, a more mature app, and a proven track record since 2019. Saily, built by the team behind NordVPN, launched in 2024 with competitive pricing and a privacy-first approach that frequent travelers increasingly value. This comparison covers pricing, coverage, app experience, and which one wins for popular Asia destinations including Japan, Singapore, Thailand, and South Korea - so you can find the best travel eSIM for your needs.

What Is Airalo?

Airalo launched in 2019 as one of the first consumer-facing eSIM marketplaces. Based in Singapore, it was early to recognize that travelers needed a simpler alternative to roaming charges and physical SIM swaps. Today it covers 200+ destinations with local, regional, and global eSIM plans, and has processed millions of activations worldwide.

Airalo's marketplace model means it aggregates eSIMs from multiple local carriers per destination, giving you options on network and price. You buy via the Airalo app, receive a QR code or automatic eSIM push, scan it, and you're connected - no physical SIM required. The app has a 4.6 rating on iOS and has improved significantly since launch. Airalo also offers an "Alobucks" rewards program where purchases earn credits toward future plans.

What Is Saily?

Saily launched in 2024, built by NordSecurity - the same team behind NordVPN and NordPass. That pedigree matters: NordSecurity has spent years building privacy-focused consumer tech at scale, and Saily inherits that DNA with built-in tracker blocking on all plans.

Saily covers 200+ destinations and competes directly with Airalo on price, often matching or beating it for popular Asia routes. Its standout differentiators are the NordVPN-team trust factor, cleaner app interface, and privacy protection built into the connection itself - something Airalo doesn't offer natively. Saily also offers unlimited data plans in select destinations, which Airalo's basic tiers don't always match.

Coverage and Network Quality

On coverage, the airalo vs saily comparison is close. Both Airalo and Saily support 200+ destinations and use a similar model: they partner with local carriers per country and provide connectivity through those networks. In major urban destinations - Tokyo, Singapore, Seoul, Bangkok - both perform reliably on 4G LTE and increasingly on 5G where available.

Airalo has been in market longer and has a more established network of carrier partnerships, which gives it a slight edge in regional plan quality and consistency. Saily's coverage is strong in major destinations but has been reported as less consistent in rural or remote areas - expected for a platform that launched in 2024 and is still building out its carrier network.

One important note for both: eSIM plans are data-only by default. Calls and SMS typically need to use your home carrier number over WiFi (via apps like WhatsApp, FaceTime) rather than the local eSIM number. If you need a local number for two-factor authentication or restaurant reservations, check whether the specific plan includes a local number before buying.

Privacy and Security

In the airalo vs saily privacy comparison, Saily has the clear advantage for privacy-conscious travelers. Saily includes built-in tracker blocking on all plans - similar to what a VPN does for your browsing - without requiring a separate app or subscription. This means your connection on public WiFi at airports or hotels is more private by default.

Airalo does not include any built-in privacy features. If privacy matters to you on the road, you'd need to pair Airalo with a separate VPN service. The combination of Saily plus NordVPN (both from NordSecurity) gives you arguably the strongest privacy setup for travel connectivity, and many users bundle both for a discount.

For casual travelers not particularly concerned about privacy, this distinction may not matter. For digital nomads, journalists, business travelers, or anyone frequently using public networks, Saily's built-in protection is a meaningful advantage.

App Experience and Setup

Both Airalo and Saily use QR code setup - buy the plan, receive a QR code, scan it in your phone's eSIM settings, and activate before departure. The process typically takes under five minutes and works on any modern eSIM-compatible device (most iPhones since XS, most Android flagships from 2020 onward).

Airalo's app is more mature, with a larger library of user reviews and a more established support team. Ratings sit at 4.6 on iOS. The interface covers plan browsing, purchase history, data usage tracking, and the Alobucks rewards program.

Saily's app has a cleaner, more modern UI that reflects NordSecurity's design sensibility. iOS rating is 4.5. It's slightly newer and lacks some of the community review depth Airalo has accumulated, but the core booking experience is smooth and straightforward. Customer support response times are competitive.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Airalo or Saily better for Japan?
For Japan, both work well. Saily is typically marginally cheaper for Japan plans, and Japan's network infrastructure is excellent for both platforms. Either choice will give you reliable 4G connectivity in Tokyo, Kyoto, and Osaka. Check current pricing on both before buying, as it changes regularly.

Does Saily work on iPhone?
Yes, Saily is compatible with all eSIM-capable iPhones (iPhone XS and later). For setup on iPhone, both use a standard QR code scan through your iPhone's Settings > Cellular > Add eSIM menu. See our eSIM setup guide for step-by-step instructions.

Can I use Airalo or Saily for calls and SMS?
Both platforms offer data-only eSIM plans by default. Neither natively provides voice or SMS on their basic plans. For calls and SMS, you can use your home carrier number over WiFi via apps like WhatsApp, FaceTime, or Google Voice. Some plans include a local number - check the specific plan details before purchasing.

Is Saily trustworthy for travel?
Yes. Saily is built by NordSecurity, the company behind NordVPN, which has a strong track record in consumer privacy and security. In any airalo vs saily trust comparison, both are legitimate platforms, though Airalo has more established user reviews given its longer history.

What happens if my Airalo or Saily eSIM doesn't work?
Both platforms offer customer support for connectivity issues. Airalo allows refunds if the eSIM is unused and uninstalled. Saily offers refunds for unused eSIMs within 30 days. In both cases, contact support through the app before arrival at your destination if possible.

Are there promo codes for Airalo or Saily?
Both platforms run occasional promotions. Check all travel tools on Catavel for the latest deals, or search for current referral codes on their respective apps.

How does Saily compare to NordVPN for travel security?
Saily and NordVPN serve different purposes but complement each other. Saily is a travel eSIM (mobile data connectivity) with built-in tracker blocking. NordVPN is a full VPN service that encrypts all your internet traffic. For maximum privacy on public WiFi, using both together is the strongest combination. Whichever you pick in the airalo vs saily decision, pairing your eSIM with a VPN adds another layer of protection.
