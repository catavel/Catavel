AirAsia MOVE App - Where the Best Deals Live

The AirAsia MOVE app (formerly AirAsia Super App) is where most of the best promotional deals and app-exclusive fares appear first. A few things to know:

  • New user bonus - first-time app bookers in Malaysia get up to 35% + RM20 off with no minimum spend. Singapore users get up to SGD 24 off app bookings.
  • Push notifications - enable these to catch 72-hour flash sales. The lowest seats sell out fast, often within hours of announcement.
  • Flight + hotel bundles - the SNAP (flight + hotel) package on the app gives up to RM500 off when booked together, which is substantially better than booking separately.
  • In-flight meal pre-order - ordering meals via the app before your flight saves up to 25% vs ordering onboard.

AirAsia Free Seats Promo - How It Actually Works

The "Free Seats" promotion is AirAsia's signature campaign - base fares of 0 MYR, 0 THB, or 0 PHP on select routes. Here's what "free" actually means:

  • The base fare is zero, but you still pay airport taxes, fuel surcharges, and any selected add-ons (baggage, seat, meals)
  • Total all-in cost for a domestic route during a Free Seats promo is typically RM30-80 depending on taxes and route
  • International Free Seats routes have higher taxes - a "free" KL to Singapore fare may still cost SGD 30-50 all-in
  • Seats are very limited - the lowest base-fare seats are usually gone within the first hour of a sale going live
  • Book via the MOVE app for the fastest checkout experience during high-traffic sale periods
Catavel Tip: During Free Seats sales, go straight to the route you want rather than browsing. The search results sort by price but the lowest seats disappear fast. Have your payment details saved in the app - the checkout process is competitive.

SNAP - AirAsia's Flight + Hotel Bundle Explained

SNAP is AirAsia's flight-plus-hotel package offering and one of the better-value bundles in the region. When you book flight and hotel together through SNAP, the total price is often lower than booking each separately.

  • Available via the AirAsia MOVE app under the "SNAP" tab
  • Current discount up to RM500 off packages with no minimum spend
  • Works best for popular routes: KL to Bangkok, SG to Bali, MNL to Cebu with hotel
  • The flight component uses AirAsia fares, hotel inventory is from partner OTAs
  • Compare SNAP total price vs booking Klook hotels or Trip.com separately to confirm it's the better deal

Credit Card Combos for AirAsia - SG and MY

AirAsia Add-Ons - When to Buy vs Skip

AirAsia's revenue model relies on add-ons. Knowing which to buy and which to skip saves money without sacrificing comfort.

  • Baggage - always buy upfront with your flight booking. Baggage purchased at the airport is significantly more expensive than at-booking prices.
  • Seat selection - skip for short regional routes unless you have a strong preference. The AirAsia "Seat Together" feature in the app lets groups sit together automatically.
  • In-flight meals - worth pre-ordering via app (up to 25% discount vs onboard). Skip if you're on a route under 2 hours.
  • Travel insurance - AirAsia's bundled insurance is basic. For longer trips or higher-cost flights, standalone travel insurance from a dedicated insurer typically offers better coverage.
  • Value Pack - AirAsia's pre-bundled add-on (meal + baggage + insurance) saves 10-30% vs buying each separately during sale periods. Worth it if you need all three components.

Frequently Asked Questions - AirAsia Promo Code

Where do I enter an AirAsia promo code?
Enter the code in the "Promo Code" field during checkout, after selecting your flight and before payment. The discount will apply to the eligible flight segment automatically. If the code doesn't work, check that the route and travel dates fall within the promotion's terms.

Why is the AirAsia promo code not working?
Common reasons: the code has expired, the route you selected is excluded, you're not meeting the minimum spend, or the promo seats for that code are sold out. AirAsia allocates a limited number of seats at the promo price - when those seats are gone, the code no longer applies even if the promotion is technically active.

Can I use an AirAsia promo code on the MOVE app?
Yes, promo codes work on both the website and the MOVE app. The app sometimes surfaces additional app-exclusive deals that can't be found on the website, so it's worth checking both if you're price-comparing.

What is AirAsia BIG Loyalty and is it worth joining?
BIG Loyalty (formerly BIG Points) is AirAsia's rewards program. Members earn XP Points for every SGD 0.30 or MYR equivalent spent. Points can be redeemed for flight upgrades, add-ons, and hotel deals. It's free to join and worth doing - the accumulated points from a few flights add up to meaningful discounts on future bookings.

When is the next AirAsia Free Seats promo?
AirAsia runs Free Seats promotions multiple times a year, with major windows in February-March (the BIG Sale) and again in November-December. Smaller 72-hour flash sales with deeply discounted seats happen almost weekly. Subscribe to the AirAsia newsletter and enable MOVE app push notifications to catch them.

Is AirAsia SNAP worth it compared to booking separately?
It depends on the route and travel dates. SNAP packages work best for popular leisure routes (KL-Bangkok, SG-Bali) where hotel demand is predictable. For niche destinations or if you need a specific hotel, booking separately on Trip.com or Klook may give you better hotel options at a comparable total price. Always compare before committing.

Do AirAsia promo codes work on AirAsia X (long-haul) flights?
Some do, some don't - it depends on the specific promotion. The major annual sales (BIG Sale, Year-End) typically include both AirAsia (AK, FD) and AirAsia X (D7) routes. Code-specific promotions sometimes restrict to one or the other. Check the promo terms for the D7 flight code inclusion.
