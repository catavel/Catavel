Singapore Pick-Up Points Guide

SG-Malaysia buses depart from several hubs in Singapore. Choosing the right one saves time:

  • Golden Mile Complex (Beach Road): Largest concentration of SG-MY operators. Operators including Transtar, KKKL, StarMart, and Grassland depart from here. Take buses 14, 65, or 980 from Bugis MRT. Cheapest area for last-minute walk-in tickets but online pre-booking saves the queuing.
  • Lavender Street (Kallang area): Closer to the MRT (Lavender station, EW line), some operators offer departures from here as an alternative to Golden Mile. Check specific operator pick-up point when booking.
  • City Plaza (Geylang): Eastern Singapore pick-up point for operators serving travellers from the East. Accessible from Aljunied MRT.
  • One KM Mall (Paya Lebar): Some operators offer Paya Lebar area pick-up. Check when booking if this suits your location.
Catavel Tip: For SG to KL trips, arrive at Golden Mile at least 20 minutes before departure. Immigration at the Woodlands Checkpoint adds variable time - morning departures before 8AM and midday departures between 11AM-2PM tend to clear faster than evening rush hours. Budget 30-45 extra minutes on Friday afternoons and eve of public holidays.

Cross-Border Travel Tips - Customs and Immigration

  • Singapore exit at Woodlands or Tuas: All buses stop at the Singapore checkpoint first. You alight, clear Singapore immigration, re-board the bus, then clear Malaysian immigration at Johor Bahru (JB Checkpoint or CIQ). The bus waits for passengers at both stops.
  • Documents needed: Valid passport (6 months validity recommended), return/onward ticket if required, any applicable Malaysian visa. Singapore PR holders: bring your blue IC and re-entry permit.
  • Avoid peak crossing times: Friday evenings, Sunday evenings, eve of Malaysian and Singapore public holidays see the longest queues. The Causeway adds 1-3 hours during these windows.
  • Luggage: Standard allowance varies by operator. Most SG-MY coaches allow 20kg checked bag plus cabin bag. Transtar and KKKL have clear luggage policies on their booking pages.
  • Dress code at customs: No specific dress code but remove belts, watches, and items from pockets before immigration counters to speed up security.

Frequently Asked Questions - BusOnlineTicket Promo Code

Where do I enter a BusOnlineTicket promo code?
After selecting your bus and seats, on the booking summary page, look for the "Use Reward / Discount Code?" link. Click it to reveal the input field, enter your code, and click Apply. The discount updates in the fare breakdown immediately. The code field is not on the search results page - only on the booking summary before payment.

Is BusOnlineTicket safe and reliable?
Yes - BusOnlineTicket has been operating since 2007 and is one of Southeast Asia's most established bus ticketing portals, covering 8,000+ routes across 200+ operators. Tickets are confirmed via email and the app. If a bus is cancelled by the operator, BusOnlineTicket processes refunds or rebooking. Can I get a refund if I cancel my BusOnlineTicket booking?
Refund eligibility depends on the operator's policy selected at booking. Some operators offer full refunds up to 24 hours before departure, others are non-refundable. Adding GoFlexi at checkout lets you change your travel date without amendment fees, which is useful when plans are uncertain. Always check the cancellation terms on the booking summary before confirming.

What is GoFlexi on BusOnlineTicket?
GoFlexi is an add-on purchased at booking that allows you to change your travel date and time without paying amendment fees. It's particularly useful for uncertain trip plans or bookings during peak seasons when you want to lock in a seat early but may need flexibility. The cost of GoFlexi varies by route and operator.

How early should I book SG-KL bus tickets?
For weekday travel, 1-3 days ahead is usually sufficient. For Friday departures and long weekends, book 1-2 weeks ahead as popular operators (Transtar, KKKL) sell out quickly. During Hari Raya and Chinese New Year, book 3-4 weeks ahead or earlier - these are the most congested travel periods of the year on the SG-MY corridor.

Does BusOnlineTicket cover ferry tickets?
Yes - BusOnlineTicket also covers ferry routes including Batam and Bintan ferries from Singapore, and inter-island ferries in Malaysia. Apply your BusOnlineTicket promo code the same way as bus bookings. Ferry tickets are often cheaper bought through BusOnlineTicket than directly at the ferry terminal.

Is the bus cheaper than flying from Singapore to KL?
On base fare, yes - bus tickets from SGD $19 vs budget flights from SGD $40-120. Door-to-door time is the real tradeoff: bus takes 5-7 hours total including immigration stops, while flying with airport time takes 3-4 hours. For day trips or heavy luggage, using a BusOnlineTicket promo code on the bus wins on total cost (no baggage fees). For business or time-sensitive travel, the flight wins on speed.
