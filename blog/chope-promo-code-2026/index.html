What Happened to ChopeDeals? - Grab Dine Out Explained

In July 2024, Grab acquired Chope. As part of this integration, dining deals previously offered under ChopeDeals have been consolidated into Grab Dine Out. Here's what changed and what stayed the same:

  • What moved to Grab: Pre-paid dining vouchers and deal packages that were under ChopeDeals are now accessible via the Grab app under the "Dine Out" section. If you can't find a deal on the Chope website, check Grab Dine Out.
  • What stayed on Chope: Restaurant reservations, ChopePerks loyalty rewards, 1-for-1 Chope Exclusives, and off-peak dining discounts remain on the Chope platform.
  • For Grab users: GrabRewards points can now be applied to some Dine Out voucher purchases. If you're already a regular Grab user, check Dine Out in the Grab app for the best current dining deals.
  • Practical tip: Use Chope for reservations and loyalty points, use Grab Dine Out for pre-paid vouchers and steep percentage-off deals at specific restaurants.

ChopePerks Rewards - How to Earn and Redeem

ChopePerks is Chope's loyalty program, built around Chope-Dollars:

  • Earn at least 100 Chope-Dollars per fulfilled reservation made via chope.co or the Chope app
  • Earn rate varies by membership tier - higher tiers earn more per reservation
  • Chope-Dollars redeem for: dining vouchers, KrisFlyer miles (Singapore Airlines), discount codes, and lifestyle rewards via the ChopePerks rewards catalogue
  • The KrisFlyer redemption is particularly useful for travelers - dining out in Singapore earns you air miles
  • New sign-ups get 100 Chope-Dollars on first reservation; referral link sign-ups get 300
  • Campaign codes (posted on stories.chope.co) award bonus Chope-Dollars on top of the standard earn rate

1-for-1 Dining Deals on Chope

The 1-for-1 deals available through Chope Exclusives are some of the best recurring food deals in Singapore. How they work:

  • Book through the Chope app or website and select restaurants tagged as "Chope Exclusives"
  • The 1-for-1 applies to signature dishes - essentially buy one main course, get one free
  • Available at rotating restaurants across cuisines - Japanese, Korean, Western, local, and more
  • Must book through Chope to unlock the deal - walk-ins do not qualify
  • Off-peak slot bookings sometimes unlock additional discounts on top of 1-for-1 pricing

Credit Cards That Stack Well with Chope

The best dining savings in Singapore come from combining Chope deals with the right credit card:

  • American Express (general dining): Amex cards historically have strong dining partnerships in Singapore. Check your card's dining privileges page for participating Chope restaurants.
  • OCBC dining cards: OCBC's dining-focused cards offer additional cashback or discounts at participating restaurants. Worth checking OCBC's restaurant partner list against Chope's inventory.
  • UOB dining cards: UOB's Dining Dollars program gives cashback on restaurant spend including Chope-booked meals. Combine with Chope 1-for-1 for compounding savings.

  • Citi cashback cards: Citi's dining cashback applies broadly to restaurant spend, including meals reserved via Chope at participating restaurants.

Frequently Asked Questions - Chope Promo Code

Where do I enter a Chope promo code?
During the reservation flow, not at a checkout page. After selecting your restaurant, date, time, and party size, click "Book Now". On the reservation details page, look for the "Use a promotion code" button below the Message section. Click it, enter your code, and confirm. The code applies before you finalize the booking.

What are Chope-Dollars worth?
Chope-Dollars are the currency of the ChopePerks loyalty program. Redemption value varies by what you exchange them for - dining vouchers, KrisFlyer miles, or discount codes. Check the ChopePerks rewards catalogue in the app for current redemption rates. The KrisFlyer miles redemption is popular with Singapore Airlines frequent flyers who want to earn miles through dining.

Did ChopeDeals close down?
Not exactly - following Grab's acquisition of Chope in July 2024, ChopeDeals vouchers and dining deals were integrated into Grab Dine Out. Restaurant reservations and ChopePerks rewards continue on the Chope platform. For pre-paid dining vouchers, check the Grab app under Dine Out. For table reservations and loyalty points, use Chope directly.

How do I find the current Chope campaign promo code?
Check stories.chope.co - this is Chope's official blog where campaign codes are posted when active. Chope also sends codes via email to registered users, so signing up with your actual email address ensures you receive them directly. Campaign codes are not permanent - they run for limited periods tied to specific promotions.

Is Chope available outside Singapore?
Yes - Chope covers restaurants in Singapore, Bali, Jakarta, Bangkok, Phuket, Hong Kong, and mainland China (Shanghai, Beijing). However, the deals, rewards structure, and promo codes vary significantly by market. This article focuses on Singapore. For Hong Kong, check chope.co/hong-kong-restaurants for local deals.

Can I earn KrisFlyer miles through Chope?
Yes - Chope-Dollars earned through reservations can be redeemed for KrisFlyer miles via the ChopePerks rewards catalogue. This is one of the more unusual and useful ways to earn Singapore Airlines miles in Singapore without flying - simply by making restaurant reservations through Chope and fulfilling them.

Which credit card gives the most savings when dining with Chope?
It depends on your card. For dining-specific cashback, UOB Dining Dollars and OCBC dining cards are strong. For broader restaurant spend, Citi cashback cards and Amex dining partnerships add value. Stack your card cashback with a Chope 1-for-1 deal or off-peak discount for the best combination - this is how frequent Singapore diners typically get the most from their Chope promo code and card rewards together.
