How to Apply an Expedia Promo Code Singapore

  3. On the checkout page, locate the "Promo Code" or "Coupon Code" field.
  4. Paste your code exactly as shown above - codes are case-sensitive.

Frequently Asked Questions - Expedia Promo Code Singapore

Can I combine multiple Expedia promo codes in one booking?
No - Expedia allows only one promo code per booking. You cannot stack two discount codes. However, your bank may offer additional cashback on top of the Expedia discount depending on your card's travel rewards program.

Do I need to pay with a specific card for these codes to work?
Yes, all codes listed above are card-linked. You must complete payment using the required card (DBS, UOB, Citi, Amex, SCB, HSBC, YouTrip, or Visa) for the discount to apply. Expedia verifies the card type at checkout - paying with a different card will invalidate the code.

What should I do if my Expedia promo code is not working?
Check the following: Is the code entered exactly as shown (case-sensitive)? Are you using the correct card? Is the hotel you selected part of the "selected hotels" eligible list? Has the code expired? For DBS50 and CITI2026, verify the expiry date. If all conditions are met, try clearing your browser cache or switching to the Expedia mobile app.

Are these Expedia promo codes valid for flights and activities, or hotels only?
All codes listed in this article are hotel-specific. Expedia occasionally runs separate promo codes for flights and activities, but the card-linked bank codes (DBS, UOB, Citi, etc.) typically apply to hotel bookings only. Check Expedia's promotions page for any active flight deals.

Can I use an Expedia Singapore promo code to book hotels in other countries?
Yes - codes like YOUTRIP10, HSBC16, and the bank card codes work on worldwide hotel inventory, not just properties in Singapore. You can use them to book hotels in Bali, Bangkok, Tokyo, London, and other destinations through Expedia.com.sg.

How often are Expedia promo codes Singapore updated?
Bank-linked codes (DBSSG8, UOBSG8, CITI8) tend to stay active for months and renew quarterly. Flash deals like DBS50 and CITI2026 are campaign-specific and expire within days or weeks.
