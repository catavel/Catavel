Fly Fairly Review 2026 (Working)

By Catavel Published 2026-05-13 Updated 2026-05-13

Fly Fairly is not what most travelers expect when they first hear about it. It isn't a carbon-first platform, and it isn't a traditional OTA. It's primarily a flight booking platform built around buy now pay later (BNPL) payment options - with an optional carbon offset add-on that makes it stand out from standard booking sites. This review covers everything you need to know about Fly Fairly for 2026: how it works, pricing comparison, the carbon offset mechanism, and who it's actually best for.

What is Fly Fairly?

Fly Fairly is a global flight booking platform that combines two things most booking sites don't offer together: flexible buy now pay later payment options across 100+ payment methods, and optional carbon offset add-ons at checkout. Key facts:

  • Founded: Australia-based platform serving global markets including Singapore, Malaysia, and the Philippines
  • Rating: 4.9 star rating from verified users
  • Coverage: Global and regional airlines, every route and destination
  • Payment methods: 100+ options including Afterpay, Klarna, Affirm, Zip, Atome, credit cards, e-wallets, and cryptocurrency
  • Carbon offset: Optional add-on at checkout via CarbonClick, supporting projects certified by Gold Standard and Verra VCS
  • Add-ons available: eSIM data, baggage delay insurance, lounge access, booking flexibility protection
Catavel Tip: The platform's core value is in the payment flexibility, not the carbon offset. If you need to book a flight now but prefer to spread payments across weeks via Afterpay or Klarna, the platform solves that problem in a way Aviasales, Trip.com, and Google Flights cannot. The carbon offset is optional - add it if you want, skip it if you don't.

How Fly Fairly Works - The Booking Process

  1. Search for flights by entering your origin, destination, and dates. It searches across global and regional airlines the same way Aviasales or Skyscanner does.
  2. Select your preferred flight from the results. Fare classes, airlines, and pricing display normally.
  3. At checkout, choose your payment method from 100+ options. If selecting a BNPL option (Afterpay, Klarna, Zip, Affirm), your first payment is made now and the balance is split across weeks or months automatically.
  4. Optionally add carbon offsetting via CarbonClick. The cost is calculated based on your specific flight's estimated emissions and added to your total.
  5. Optionally add travel extras: eSIM for mobile data, baggage delay cover, lounge access, or booking flexibility protection.
  6. Receive booking confirmation and a tracking link for your carbon offset project (if selected).

Buy Now Pay Later Flights - How the BNPL Options Work

This is the platform's primary differentiator. Available BNPL providers vary by market but include:

  • Afterpay: Split into 4 equal fortnightly payments. Available to Australian and SG-adjacent markets. No interest if paid on time.
  • Klarna: Pay in 3 or pay in 30 days options. Popular in SG and MY where Klarna has expanded. No interest on qualifying purchases.
  • Zip: Split across weekly or fortnightly payments. Zip operates in Australia and select Asian markets.
  • Affirm: Monthly installments with fixed terms, common for higher-value ticket purchases.
  • Atome: 3-month split, zero interest. Available in Singapore, Malaysia, Philippines, Hong Kong.
  • Cryptocurrency: For travelers who prefer settling in digital currency - unusual for flight booking.
Catavel Tip: For Singapore and Malaysia travelers, Atome's 3-month 0% split is the cleanest BNPL option available. It requires no credit check for qualifying amounts and splits your flight cost into three equal monthly payments with no interest. Useful for locking in a low fare today without the full upfront hit on your bank account.

Carbon Offset Add-On - How It Works

The platform partners with CarbonClick to offer optional carbon offset purchases at checkout. Key things to know:

  • Certification standards: Projects are certified by Gold Standard, Verra VCS (the world's most-used greenhouse gas crediting program), ACR, and CAR - the most credible independent standards in the carbon credit market
  • Cost: Calculated based on the estimated emissions for your specific flight (route, aircraft type, cabin class). Shorter regional flights cost less to offset than long-haul.
  • Traceability: You receive a tracking link and project details - you can see exactly which certified project your money supports
  • Offset vs reduce: CarbonClick explicitly states that offsetting is only part of the answer. Reducing emissions first (efficient routing, lighter packing, economy class) is preferable before offsetting what remains
  • Payment flexibility: Carbon offsets can be paid via the same BNPL method as your flight - no separate transaction needed
  • CORSIA context: From 2027, mandatory carbon offsetting becomes global for most international flights under the ICAO CORSIA scheme. Fly Fairly's voluntary offset approach lets eco-conscious travelers act ahead of this regulatory requirement

Singapore SAF Levy - Why Carbon-Conscious Booking Matters More Now

From October 2026, Singapore will implement a Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF) levy on flights departing Changi Airport. This makes the carbon offset conversation timely for SG-based travelers:

  • The SAF levy adds S$1 to S$41.60 per ticket depending on cabin class and destination band - applied to tickets sold from April 1, 2026 for flights from October 2026 onwards
  • Budget carriers (Scoot, AirAsia) will pass 100% of the levy to passengers; full-service carriers may absorb part
  • CORSIA, the international aviation carbon scheme, becomes mandatory for virtually all international flights from 2027 onwards
  • Fly Fairly's voluntary offset approach lets travelers act on flight emissions today, before regulatory mandates make it standard practice across the industry

Who Should Use Fly Fairly

  • BNPL users: If you prefer spreading flight costs across Afterpay, Klarna, or Atome payments rather than one upfront charge - it is one of the few flight booking platforms that does this cleanly
  • Eco-conscious travelers: Those who want a certified, traceable carbon offset with their flight booking without using a separate platform
  • Corporate travelers: Companies with sustainability reporting requirements can use the platform's offset receipts as documentation
  • Tech-comfortable users: Crypto payment option suits travelers who hold digital currencies and prefer using them for travel spend

When to Use Aviasales or Trip.com Instead

  • Price-first searches: Aviasales is a meta-search engine that aggregates from hundreds of sources - it may surface lower fares for price-sensitive bookings
  • Bank card discounts on flights: Trip.com offers 3% off flights with any Visa card (valid until Jan 2027) - a straightforward discount not available on this platform
  • Bundled flight + hotel: Trip.com and Expedia offer package discounts when you book both together, which it doesn't provide
  • Loyalty points: Booking direct with an airline or through a travel credit card-linked OTA earns miles and points that the platform doesn't replicate

Frequently Asked Questions - Fly Fairly

Is Fly Fairly legitimate and safe to book through?
Yes - the platform holds a 4.9 star verified rating and has documented positive customer service experiences including handling complex requests like DPNA (Disabled Passenger with Assistance) coordination with Singapore Airlines. It processes payments through established BNPL providers (Afterpay, Klarna, Zip) which have their own consumer protections. Book via the official site at flyfairly.com.

Does Fly Fairly have promo codes for flights?
This platform doesn't regularly distribute public promo codes in the same way as Trip.com or Klook. Its value proposition is payment flexibility (BNPL) and optional carbon offsets, not code-based discounts. For traditional flight discounts, combine Aviasales (meta-search for lowest fares) with Trip.com's Visa 3% code on the fare you find.

Is carbon offsetting through Fly Fairly effective?
The carbon offsets are certified by Gold Standard and Verra VCS - the most credible independent standards globally, used by major corporate offset programs. CarbonClick provides full traceability with a tracking link per purchase. That said, carbon offsetting is a supplement to, not a replacement for, reducing emissions. Fly lower (economy class, direct routing) before offsetting what remains.

Which BNPL options work for Singapore users on Fly Fairly?
Atome (3-month 0% split) and Klarna are both available in Singapore and compatible with checkout. Afterpay and Zip availability depends on your bank card setup. Check the payment options page at checkout for the current list - payment method availability updates as new BNPL providers are added.

How does the carbon offset cost compare to the flight price?
The offset cost is typically small relative to the flight fare. A Singapore to London return in economy might generate approximately 1,800-2,000kg CO2 equivalent. At market offset rates (roughly $10-15 per tonne), this costs around $18-30 to offset - a fraction of even a budget economy fare. The exact amount is calculated and displayed at checkout before you confirm.

Can I book any airline through Fly Fairly?
The platform covers global and regional airlines. For Southeast Asia, this includes Singapore Airlines, Scoot, AirAsia, Malaysia Airlines, Thai Airways, Philippine Airlines, and international carriers. Route coverage is broad - the platform's value is in its payment flexibility, not in exclusive access to specific airlines or fares.

Is Fly Fairly better than Aviasales for finding cheap flights?
They serve different purposes. Aviasales is a pure price-comparison meta-search engine - it's better for finding the absolute cheapest fare across all booking channels. Fly Fairly is better if you've found a fare you want and need BNPL payment flexibility, or want to add a carbon offset to the booking. Use Aviasales to find the fare, then compare whether the platform's payment terms add enough value for your situation.
