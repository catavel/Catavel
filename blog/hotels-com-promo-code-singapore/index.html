One Key Rewards - How Hotels.com's Loyalty Program Works

Hotels.com merged its old reward nights system into One Key - a shared loyalty program across Hotels.com, Expedia, and Vrbo. Key things to know:

  • OneKeyCash: The rewards currency. Earned on eligible bookings across Hotels.com, Expedia, and Vrbo. Redeemable like cash on future Pay Now bookings across all three platforms.
  • Tiers: Blue (entry), Silver, Gold, Platinum. Higher tiers unlock deeper member prices (Platinum gets 20%+ vs 10%+ for Blue), VIP Access property perks, and priority support.
  • Save Your Way feature: On eligible hotels booked with Pay Now, choose between an instant discount on the current booking or banking that same value as OneKeyCash for future use. On a S$200 booking you can take S$20 off now or bank S$20 for later.
  • Cross-platform earning: OneKeyCash earned on Expedia bookings can be redeemed on Hotels.com and vice versa. Useful if you split bookings across platforms based on price.
  • VIP Access properties: Selected hotels offer One Key members exclusive rates plus perks - free breakfast, room upgrades, spa credits. Filter for VIP Access on the Hotels.com app to see eligible properties.

How to Apply a Hotels.com Promo Code

The promo code field appears at checkout, not during the search phase:

  1. Sign in to your One Key account first - member prices only activate when logged in.
  2. Search for your hotel, select dates and room type.
  3. On the checkout page, look for "Apply a coupon" below the payment details section.
  4. Enter your Hotels.com promo code and click Apply - the discount updates in the price breakdown immediately.
  5. Complete payment with your preferred card. If combining with a bank card deal, pay with the eligible card.
Catavel Tip: Some Hotels.com promo codes and One Key member prices can be combined - the code applies on top of the already-discounted member rate. Check the code's fine print for "combinable with member prices" language. Codes that restrict stacking will say so explicitly.

Hotels.com App - Why It's Worth Downloading

The Hotels.com app offers advantages not available on the desktop site:

  • App-only prices: Selected hotels list lower rates on the app than on the website - often 5-15% cheaper for the same room type.
  • AI room recommendations: The app's recommendation engine analyzes 40+ factors (your past stays, trip purpose, group size) to suggest the best-fit rooms - useful when comparing properties in an unfamiliar destination.
  • Property comparison: Compare up to 5 hotels side-by-side including price, rating, amenities, and cancellation policy in one view.
  • Price alerts: Set alerts for specific hotels - the app notifies you when rates drop below your threshold.
  • Offline access: Booking confirmations and hotel details accessible without data connectivity - useful at airports and hotels with weak WiFi.

Frequently Asked Questions - Hotels.com Promo Code

What is the best Hotels.com promo code for Singapore right now?
The most consistent discount is the One Key member price (10% or more off) which applies automatically when you're signed in. For new members, signing up gives 10% off the first booking on top of any active campaign code. Bank card deals from DBS, UOB, and OCBC provide additional savings stacked at payment - check your bank's travel promotions page before confirming any booking above S$200.

What is One Key and is it worth joining?
One Key is Hotels.com's free loyalty program, shared with Expedia and Vrbo. Joining is free and takes under two minutes. Benefits start immediately: 10%+ member prices on 500,000+ hotels, OneKeyCash earnings on eligible bookings, and the Save Your Way feature for flexible rewards. If you book hotels even twice a year, the member pricing alone pays off within the first booking.

Can I combine a Hotels.com promo code with One Key member prices?
Sometimes yes - certain codes are explicitly combinable with member prices (the code applies on top of the already-discounted rate). Others restrict stacking. Check the fine print of any code you plan to use. Generally, bank card deals (applied at payment) can be stacked on top of both the promo code and member price discount.

How does Hotels.com's price guarantee work?
If you find a lower refundable rate for the same hotel, room type, and dates on another site, Hotels.com will match it via their Price Guarantee page. Submit the competing rate, and if verified, the difference is refunded immediately for online payment bookings or after checkout for pay-at-property bookings. The price match covers online rates only, not hotel direct phone quotes or opaque booking rates.

Is Hotels.com better than Expedia for Singapore travelers?
They share the One Key program so rewards are interchangeable. The practical difference is in current bank card deals: Expedia SG currently has a broader bank card network (OCBC, CIMB 9%, Maybank 7%) than Hotels.com SG. For hotel-only bookings where you're focusing on rewards accumulation, Hotels.com's VIP Access perks add value at select properties. For the best bank card discount on a given booking, check both platforms before confirming.

Does Hotels.com list serviced apartments and hostels, or only hotels?
Hotels.com lists a wide range of accommodation types including hotels, serviced apartments, resorts, hostels, and vacation rentals. For backpacker hostels and very budget guesthouses in Asia, Agoda tends to have deeper inventory. For serviced apartments and villa-style stays, Hotels.com competes well particularly in Europe, Australia, and Japan markets.

Where do I find the latest Hotels.com promo code for Singapore?
The best sources for current Hotels.com promo code deals are: the Hotels.com app deals section (app-only prices), your bank's travel promotions page (DBS, UOB, OCBC, HSBC), and the One Key member prices visible when signed in. Campaign codes circulate through third-party coupon sites but verify expiry dates before applying.
