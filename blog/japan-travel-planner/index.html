Why a Japan Travel Planner Needs Booking Dates

Japan runs on release dates. Many of the things travelers from Singapore, Malaysia and the Philippines want most open for sale on a fixed day, at a fixed hour, in Japan time. Miss the window and you are refreshing a sold-out page.

A plain itinerary template does not help here. It lists Day 1 to Day 7 and leaves you to work out, for every ticket, when sales open relative to your travel dates. That is the part people get wrong, because each attraction uses a different rule: some open a set number of days before, some on a set day of the previous month, one runs a lottery three months out.

If you still need the route itself, start with our 7-day Japan itinerary. This guide covers the step after that: turning the route into a booking schedule.

When to Book Each Part of a Japan Trip

These are the rules our planner uses. We checked them against official sources on 30 September 2026. Rules change, so always confirm on the official site before you pay. All times are Japan time (JST), which is 1 hour ahead of Singapore, Kuala Lumpur and Manila.

Nintendo Museum (Kyoto) Lottery only. Apply during the month that is 3 months before your visit, until 23:59 JST on its last day. Official

Doing this by hand means a calendar reminder for every row, recalculated each time your dates move. That is exactly the job we wanted a spreadsheet to do.

Catavel Tip: Two windows catch most people out. The Nintendo Museum lottery closes about 3 months before your trip, and the Ghibli Museum opens on the 10th of the month before. If you are flying in early April, that means applying for Nintendo in January and buying Ghibli tickets on 10 March.

What the Planner Does Not Do

We would rather you know the limits before you buy.

  • It does not book anything. It tells you when to book and links to where. You still buy each ticket yourself.
  • It does not send notifications. It is a spreadsheet, not an app. You open it to see what is due. Pair it with one weekly calendar reminder to check the Checklist.
  • Rules can change. We recheck the booking rules regularly, but an attraction can change its sales date without notice. Confirm on the official site before you pay.
  • The Today tab is compact. Long text gets cut short with "..." to fit a phone. The full details stay in Itinerary and Research.
  • Excel is a snapshot for deals. The rest of the planner works the same in Excel, but the deals list only refreshes in Google Sheets.
