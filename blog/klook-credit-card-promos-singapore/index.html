Best Credit Cards for Klook in Singapore 2026

Not all credit cards are equal when it comes to Klook. Here is a breakdown of which cards consistently deliver the best Klook savings for Singapore users.

DBS / POSB Cards - Best Overall

DBS has the most comprehensive Klook partnership of any Singapore bank. The Saturday flash deals (up to 50% off, codes drop at 12pm) are the biggest discounts you will find on the platform. Even outside of Saturdays, DBS/POSB cardholders enjoy a standing 7% off sitewide with minimum spend S$200. If you only have one card in your wallet, a DBS or POSB card gives you the most Klook coverage year-round.

DBS also runs dedicated promos on specific categories - Shinkansen tickets (15% off with DBS/POSB), hotel deals, and occasional 50% flash deals on selected attractions. The year-round monthly code format is DBS267OFF + month abbreviation, so DBS267OFFMAY for May.

Citibank Mastercard - Best for One-Off Bookings

The Citi promo is straightforward: 7% off with minimum spend S$250, capped at S$20. Valid until 30 June 2026 and covers everything sitewide including attractions, tours, hotels, and transport. Citibank also participates in Mastercard's broader seasonal campaigns on Klook, so Citi Mastercard holders sometimes get stacked discounts during major sale events.

Maybank Cards - Best for Malaysia Trips

Maybank stands out specifically for travelers who frequently cross into Malaysia. The SGMBB15 code gives 15% off Malaysia bookings (no minimum spend, capped S$25) - a strong deal if you are planning a Johor Bahru day trip or a weekend in KL. The standard monthly Maybank code also gives 7% off sitewide with minimum spend S$200.

Trust Card - Best for New Card Holders

Trust Card is a newer player but punches above its weight for Klook. The sign-up bonus alone is a S$80 Klook e-gift card when you apply via Klook's dedicated Trust Card link. On top of that, Trust cardholders get 7% off monthly with minimum spend S$220 (capped S$17). If you were already considering a new card, this sign-up deal makes Trust worth a look.

OCBC Cards - Solid Backup Option

OCBC typically offers 7% off with minimum spend S$250 (capped S$20) through a monthly code available on Klook's Payment Discounts page. It is not the most exciting deal but it is reliable, especially during months when DBS Saturday codes run out fast.

UOB Cards - Best for Theme Park Fans

UOB regularly runs 1-for-1 theme park flash deals on Klook (Universal Studios Beijing, Shanghai Disneyland). If theme parks are your thing, keep UOB's Klook promo page bookmarked. The standing sitewide deal is S$10 off via monthly code.

How to Stack Klook Promo Codes with Credit Card Discounts

This is where most travelers leave money on the table. Klook allows you to stack a platform promo code with a card-linked payment discount on the same booking. Here is the correct order of operations:

  1. Pick your activity and add it to cart
  2. At checkout, enter your platform promo code first (e.g. a seasonal Klook code)
  3. Select your eligible bank card as payment method
  4. The card-linked discount applies automatically on top
  5. If you have KlookCash, apply it last to stack a third layer
Catavel Tip: You cannot combine two platform promo codes on one booking. But a platform code plus a bank card discount is allowed - this is the sweet spot. For example: apply a seasonal 10% code, then pay with your DBS card for an additional 7% off.

One thing to watch: some specific promotions explicitly exclude card discounts. Always read the promo terms before booking. If a promotion says "not valid with other offers," that usually means no stacking with another platform code - but card-linked discounts may still apply.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which credit card gives the best Klook discount in Singapore?
DBS and POSB cards offer the widest coverage - combining Saturday flash deals (up to 50% off) with a year-round 7% off sitewide deal. For a single large booking, Citibank Mastercard (7% off, min. S$250, capped S$20) is also highly competitive. The right answer depends on your spending habits and which bank cards you already hold.

Can I use a Klook credit card promo and a regular promo code together?
Yes, in most cases. Bank card discounts and platform promo codes can be stacked on the same booking. Apply the promo code first at checkout, then select your eligible bank card as payment. KlookCash can also be layered on top. The main restriction is that you cannot combine two platform promo codes on a single booking.

Do Klook credit card promos work for international bookings?
Most sitewide bank promos cover both Singapore and overseas bookings on Klook. However, some promos are destination-specific - for example, Maybank's SGMBB15 code targets Malaysia bookings only. Always check the promo terms for eligible destinations before confirming your booking.

Are Klook credit card promos valid for hotels?
Yes. Most sitewide bank promos (DBS, Citi, OCBC, Trust) cover hotels booked through Klook. Note that hotels listed on third-party platforms aggregated by Klook (such as Agoda or Booking.com) may be excluded. Hotels booked directly on Klook's own inventory are eligible.

How do I find the latest Klook bank promo codes?
Go to Klook's website, click on "Payment Discounts" on the homepage (or search for your bank name in Klook's promo section). Monthly codes from DBS, Maybank, Citi, and others are listed there. They refresh on the 1st of each month and quantities are limited, so check early in the month.

Do Klook credit card promos stack with KlookCash?
Yes. KlookCash (earned from previous bookings) can be applied on top of both a platform promo code and a bank card discount. Apply your promo code first, then select your bank card, then apply KlookCash at the final payment step. This three-layer stack is the maximum savings method available on Klook.

Are there Klook promos for foreign credit cards?
Most bank promos listed on this page are for Singapore-issued cards only. However, network-level promos (Mastercard, Visa) may be available to international cardholders. Visa weekends occasionally offer S$10 off plus an extra 15% to eligible Visa cardholders. Check Klook's Payment Discounts page for current Visa and Mastercard network promos.
