How to Use a Klook Promo Code

  1. Find your activity on Klook and tap "Book Now"
  2. Select your date, options, and add to cart
  3. On the checkout page, enter your code in the "Promo Code" field
  4. Tap "Apply" and the saving appears on your total before payment

Some deals do not need a code. The discount shows automatically on the activity page - you will see the original price crossed out and the new price displayed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do Klook promo codes expire?

Yes, most codes have expiry dates. Campaign codes like SPRINGSEAATT expire April 12, while bank codes typically rotate monthly. Catavel updates this page weekly to remove expired codes and add new ones.

Can I use multiple Klook promo codes on one booking?

No, Klook allows only one promo code per transaction. Pick the code that gives you the highest discount for your booking amount and apply that one.

Why is my Klook promo code not working?

Common reasons: the code expired, you have not met the minimum spend, the code is restricted to a specific market (e.g., Singapore only), or it is for first-time users and you have booked before. Try switching your Klook region in the top-right menu.

Does Klook offer hotel discount codes?

Yes. NEWMARHOTEL20 gives SGD 15 off hotels with a minimum spend of SGD 150. Credit card codes like DBS267OFFMAR also apply to hotel bookings made through Klook.

What is the best Klook promo code right now?

For new users, KLOOKNEW (10% off) is the best entry point. For returning users, credit card codes like CITIM267OFFMAR (7% off) or ADAMANDLINDSKLOOK (5% off theme parks, valid until Dec 2026) offer the strongest savings.

Are there Klook app-exclusive vouchers?

Yes, Klook occasionally offers app-exclusive codes. Download the Klook app and check the "Promos" section for current app-only deals that are not available on the desktop site.
