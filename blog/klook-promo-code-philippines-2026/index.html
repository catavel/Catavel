Frequently Asked Questions - Klook Promo Code Philippines

Does the GCash 10% Klook deal work for domestic Philippines bookings?
Yes - the GCash 10% sitewide deal applies to all destinations including local Philippines experiences. The minimum spend is P8,000 and the cap is P1,000 discount. For domestic activities like Boracay island hopping or Palawan tours, you may need to bundle multiple bookings to reach the P8,000 threshold.

Can I pay for Klook in Philippine Peso?
Yes. Klook displays prices in PHP for Philippine users and processes GCash, Maya, BDO, BPI, Metrobank, UnionBank, Visa, Mastercard, Amex, and JCB payments. Some international experiences are priced in USD or HKD - Klook converts at checkout using a current exchange rate.

How early should I book Klook activities for Philippines trips?
For popular domestic destinations: Palawan Underground River requires 2-4 weeks advance booking as daily entries are capped. El Nido private tours during peak season (Holy Week, summer, December) sell out 1-2 weeks ahead. For Boracay and Cebu, 3-7 days advance is usually sufficient. International bookings (Japan, Korea) benefit from 2-3 weeks lead time for the best seat and time slot selection.

What should I do if my Klook promo code isn't working?
Check: Is the code for your specific market (some codes are SGD or HKD market only)? Is the activity eligible (some exclusions apply)? Has the code expired? For bank card deals, are you paying with the eligible card? If all conditions are met, try the code on the Klook app instead of the website - some codes are app-only.

Are Klook bookings refundable for Filipino users?
Refund eligibility depends on the specific product. Klook shows cancellation terms clearly on each listing - look for "Free cancellation until [date]" labels. Most popular Philippines experiences (island hopping, tours) offer free cancellation up to 24 hours before. Non-refundable bookings are clearly marked. If an operator cancels, Klook provides a full refund or credit.

Does Klook have a Philippine customer service number?
Klook's primary support channels for PH users are in-app chat (fastest response), email at support@klook.com, and their Help Center at klook.com/en-PH/blog/help-center. For urgent issues during travel (e.g., a tour not showing up), the in-app chat connects to a live agent and is the fastest resolution path.

Can I use a Klook promo code on hotel bookings?
Some codes apply to hotels, some are activities-only. The product page will show whether your code applies before you confirm. Klook's hotel inventory in the Philippines includes Boracay and Palawan resorts at competitive rates. For international hotel bookings, Trip.com or Expedia may offer better pricing - compare before committing to your Klook promo code Philippines booking.
