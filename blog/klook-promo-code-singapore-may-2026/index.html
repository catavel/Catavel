Klook Promo Code Singapore - Best Times to Book

Klook campaigns peak during Singapore school holidays (March, June, September, December) and around major sales events like 11.11, Black Friday, and National Day in August. During these windows, expect 20-30% off on popular attractions instead of the usual 5-15%.

Flash sales typically drop at 10am SGT and sell out within hours. Following Klook Singapore on social media is the easiest way to catch these. For weekday travelers, certain attractions offer lower base prices on non-peak days, so check both options before booking.

If you are planning multiple bookings across the year, it pays to track Klook's quarterly campaign cadence. The first quarter (January to March) sees Lunar New Year promotions and Spring travel deals to Japan, Korea, and Taiwan. The second quarter (April to June) is dominated by Mother's Day and June school holiday campaigns, with stronger discounts for family bundles. Third quarter (July to September) brings the Singapore Great Sale and Hari Raya Haji travel deals. Fourth quarter (October to December) is the biggest, with 11.11, Black Friday, Cyber Monday, and year-end clearance overlapping into deep December discounts.
