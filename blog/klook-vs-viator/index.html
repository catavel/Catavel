What Is Klook?

Klook launched in Hong Kong in 2014 and quickly became the dominant activity booking platform in Asia. With over 500,000 activities, tours, transport passes, and travel services across 170+ destinations, Klook covers everything from Universal Studios Japan tickets to JR Passes, Grab vouchers, and local SIM cards.

What sets Klook apart is its mobile-first design and deep Asia coverage. Most bookings deliver an instant QR code directly to your phone - scan at the gate and you're in. No printing, no waiting at collection counters. This "scan and go" experience is particularly strong at Asia's major theme parks and transport hubs. Klook also regularly offers flash sales, Klook Credit cashback, and exclusive discounts for credit card users across Singapore, Malaysia, and Hong Kong.

Beyond tours, Klook also lets you book accommodation, airport transfers, WiFi devices, and pocket eSIMs - making it a one-stop shop for Asia trip logistics.

What Is Viator?

Viator was founded in 1995 - long before smartphones existed - and was acquired by TripAdvisor in 2014. Today it lists over 300,000 experiences across 190+ countries, with particular strength in Europe, the Americas, Australia, and the Middle East.

Viator's biggest advantage is its deep integration with TripAdvisor reviews. Every tour listing shows verified reviews from real travelers, making it easier to vet quality before booking - especially in destinations where you're less familiar with local operators. Viator also tends to have a larger selection of small-group tours and guided day trips compared to Klook, which skews more toward attraction tickets and transport products.

For travelers heading to Rome, Paris, New York, or Cape Town, Viator's network of local tour operators is hard to beat. Its desktop experience is cleaner than Klook's, and most listings offer free cancellation up to 24 hours before the activity - a flexibility Klook doesn't always match, particularly for transport products.

Coverage and Destinations

This is where the comparison becomes clearest. Klook's depth in Asia is unmatched - Japan, South Korea, Thailand, Singapore, Hong Kong, Taiwan, Malaysia, and the Philippines all have thousands of listings each, including hyper-local options you won't find elsewhere. Klook also covers transport products unique to Asia: JR Passes, Suica cards, airport express tickets, Grab packages, and city-specific tourist passes.

Viator, by contrast, has global breadth. While its Asia selection has improved significantly, it really shines in Europe (France, Italy, Spain, Greece), the Americas (US, Mexico, Peru), and the Middle East (UAE, Jordan). Major cities like Rome, New York, and London have thousands of Viator-exclusive tour operators with deep review histories.

There's strong overlap in major tourist hubs - Singapore, Tokyo, Bangkok, and Hong Kong are well-covered on both platforms. But for off-the-beaten-path Asia destinations, Klook usually has more options at better prices. For anything outside Asia, Viator's global network gives it the edge.

Cancellation and Refund Policies

When comparing klook vs viator on cancellation, Viator has a cleaner policy overall. Most Viator listings offer free cancellation up to 24 hours before the activity, and the policy is clearly labeled on each listing. For travelers who book early and value flexibility, this is a significant advantage.

Klook's cancellation terms vary more by product type. Many attraction tickets are non-refundable once booked, and transport products (train passes, airport transfers) often have strict no-refund policies. That said, Klook does offer "Free Cancellation" labels on qualifying products - look for this filter when searching if flexibility matters to you.

Bottom line: if your plans are uncertain, Viator's blanket 24-hour cancellation policy is the safer bet. If you're confident in your itinerary and booking Asia-specific products, Klook's non-refundable pricing often comes with better upfront value.

Mobile App and User Experience

On app experience, the choice comes down to mobile-first versus research-first. The app is fast, well-organized, and makes heavy use of flash sale banners, promo code fields, and Klook Credit rewards. The QR code delivery for Asia attraction tickets is seamless - book on the app, show your phone at the entrance, done. Klook's app ratings sit at 4.7 on iOS and 4.6 on Android.

Viator's app is solid but feels more like a web-to-app conversion than a native mobile-first product. Its strength is in desktop research - reading through detailed TripAdvisor-verified reviews before booking. The Viator app rates 4.7 on iOS and 4.3 on Android. For travelers who prefer to research thoroughly on desktop and book in advance, Viator's experience works well.

For the traveler who books activities on the go while already at the destination, Klook's app is the more natural choice.
