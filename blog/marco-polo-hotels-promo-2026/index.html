GHA DISCOVERY - Marco Polo's Loyalty Program

Marco Polo Hotels participates in the Global Hotel Alliance (GHA) DISCOVERY program - one of the world's largest independent hotel loyalty networks with over 800 hotels across 40+ brands:

  • DISCOVERY Dollars (D$): Earned on eligible stays, redeemable for room upgrades, free nights, dining credits, and experiences at participating GHA hotels worldwide.
  • Member rates: 10-15% off Best Available Rate on direct bookings while logged in. Rates are not visible unless you're signed in.
  • D$ earning rule: D$ earned on one stay cannot be applied to the same stay. They credit after checkout and apply to future bookings.
  • Minimum stay for promotions: Many Marco Polo promotional rates require a minimum of 2 consecutive nights. Single-night stays do not qualify for the promotional tier discount or multi-stay bonus D$.
  • Booking channel matters: OTA bookings at third-party platforms exclude full D$ earning. Always book on marcopolohotels.com or the GHA DISCOVERY app for full program eligibility.
  • Multi-Stay 2026 bonus: Complete qualifying stays across multiple GHA brands in 2026 to earn bonus DISCOVERY Dollars. Particularly valuable for frequent travelers who already rotate between GHA-affiliated properties in Asia.

Best Marco Polo Hotels in Hong Kong

All three flagship HK properties are located in Tsim Sha Tsui, Kowloon - walking distance from each other and the Star Ferry pier. Each has a distinct positioning:

  • Marco Polo Hongkong Hotel: The flagship. Victoria Harbour views, rooftop pool, multiple F&B outlets. Best for leisure travelers wanting the classic HK harbour experience. Pool is on Level 6 with views of the waterfront area.
  • Gateway Hotel, Hong Kong: Connected directly to Harbour City shopping mall via covered walkway. Best for shopping-focused stays and families who want mall access without weather exposure. Celebration packages for special events.
  • Prince Hotel, Hong Kong: More corporate positioning. Direct MTR access (Tsim Sha Tsui station), no-frills luxury for business travelers who need fast transit connections.

Best Time to Visit Hong Kong - Seasonal Pricing

Knowing HK hotel pricing patterns helps time a Marco Polo booking for best value:

  • Cheapest months: July and August (summer heat and occasional typhoons keep demand lower despite school holidays). September after typhoon season ends is also good value.
  • Peak and expensive: Chinese New Year (January-February), Golden Week (October), Christmas and New Year. Book early bird rates 2-3 months ahead for these periods.
  • Good value shoulder months: May-June and October-November (excluding Golden Week). Pleasant weather and lower rates than December-February.
  • Tip for SG travelers: Book the Bed and Breakfast package when HK hotel room-only rates are low - the included breakfast at Marco Polo properties (HKD 180-280 per person value) often makes B&B cheaper than room-only plus buying breakfast.

Frequently Asked Questions - Marco Polo Hotel Promo

What is the best Marco Polo hotel promo available now?
The early bird advance booking rate (up to 30% off) combined with a GHA DISCOVERY member rate (10-15% off, applied by logging in) gives the best stacked discount on a direct booking. The early bird works on a standalone basis and cannot be combined with other promo codes, but the member login discount applies on top. Book at marcopolohotels.com while signed in to your GHA DISCOVERY account.

What is GHA DISCOVERY and how do I join?
GHA DISCOVERY is the Global Hotel Alliance's loyalty program covering 800+ hotels across 40+ independent brands worldwide. Joining is free at ghadiscovery.com or marcopolohotels.com. Once joined, your account links to Marco Polo Hotels, Niccolo, Maqo, and hundreds of other GHA-affiliated properties. DISCOVERY Dollars earned at any GHA property can be redeemed at any other GHA property.

Can I earn GHA DISCOVERY points when booking through Agoda or Trip.com?
No - full DISCOVERY Dollar earning requires booking through official Marco Polo Hotels or GHA DISCOVERY websites. OTA bookings may qualify for a reduced earn rate but typically exclude promotions and bonus D$ programs. Always book direct if loyalty earning is a priority.

What is the difference between Marco Polo and Niccolo Hotels?
Both are Wharf Hotels brands. Marco Polo Hotels are mid-to-upper-upscale, primarily in Hong Kong and the Philippines. Niccolo Hotels are the luxury tier, located in mainland China cities (Suzhou, Chengdu, Changsha, Chongqing). Niccolo properties are typically more design-forward and higher-priced than the core Marco Polo brand. Both participate in GHA DISCOVERY and earn the same D$ currency.

Is the Marco Polo Hongkong Hotel worth the premium over Agoda alternatives?
For leisure travelers who want the harbour view and Tsim Sha Tsui location, yes. The on-site pool, proximity to the Star Ferry, and quality of F&B outlets make the total value competitive with cheaper alternatives in less central locations. Use the early bird advance booking rate and GHA DISCOVERY member rate to reduce the nightly cost, then compare the landed price against Agoda alternatives in the same zone.
