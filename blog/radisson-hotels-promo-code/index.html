How to Stack Radisson Promo Codes for Maximum Savings

Radisson's discount system allows legitimate stacking that most travelers miss:

  • Prepaid rate + member rate: In Asia Pacific, prepaid rates give up to 15% off. Log in to your Radisson Rewards account and the member discount (up to 10%) stacks on top - total up to 25% off the Best Available Rate. Non-refundable, but 25% is a meaningful saving on higher-priced properties.
  • SUMMER26 + 3,000 bonus points: The SUMMER26 code gives up to 35% off Bed and Breakfast rates plus 3,000 bonus points per stay as a Radisson Rewards member. The points don't reduce the current bill but accelerate your free night accumulation.
  • App booking + bonus points: First booking via the Radisson Hotels app with 2+ consecutive nights earns a one-time 3,000 bonus points on top of standard stay points. Good to stack on a SUMMER26 or SUITESSL booking.
  • Note on exclusions: Deals of the Day (flash sale rates) and other promotional rates typically cannot be combined with additional promo codes. When a Deals of the Day rate is active for your property, compare it against the SUMMER26-applied rate to see which is cheaper.

Radisson Rewards - How the Loyalty Program Works

Radisson Rewards is free to join and starts delivering value from the first stay:

  • Points earning: 20 points per US$1 spent on eligible stays when booking direct. Points earned on OTA bookings are lower or absent depending on the rate.
  • Redemption: Points redeem for free nights, room upgrades, dining credits, and gift cards. Redemption rates vary by property - check the Radisson Rewards catalogue before redeeming.
  • Points expiry: Points expire after 24 months of account inactivity. Earn or redeem any points within 24 months to keep the account active.
  • Tiers: Club, Silver, Gold, Platinum. Higher tiers unlock faster point earning, room upgrades, late checkout, and lounge access at participating properties.
  • Referral bonus: 1,000 bonus points per friend who completes their first stay, up to 50,000 points per year.

Radisson Brand Portfolio - Which Brand to Book

Radisson Hotel Group operates multiple brands at different price points. For Asia Pacific travelers:

  • Radisson Blu: Upper-upscale, the flagship brand. Strong business hotel offering in Bangkok, KL, and major Asian cities. Good for corporate travelers who need meeting facilities and executive lounges.
  • Radisson Collection: Luxury tier. Distinctive design-led properties in premium locations. 34 hotels globally including Asia Pacific.
  • Park Plaza: Upper midscale. Popular with leisure and business travelers in secondary city locations. Strong in Bangkok and Southeast Asia.
  • Park Inn by Radisson: Midscale. Budget-friendly Radisson brand with over 100 properties in Europe, Middle East, Africa, and Asia.
  • Country Inn and Suites: Extended stay format, currently focused on India. Good for longer corporate stays with kitchen facilities.
  • Radisson Individuals: Collection of independent boutique hotels affiliated with Radisson Rewards. Unique character properties that still earn loyalty points.

Frequently Asked Questions - Radisson Promo Code

Is the SUMMER26 code still active?
Yes - SUMMER26 booking window is open until May 28, 2026, for stays between July 1 and September 10, 2026 (London hotels from July 24). It applies to Bed and Breakfast rates at participating hotels globally. Radisson Rewards members also earn 3,000 bonus points per stay using this code. Book before May 28 to lock in the discount.

How do I apply a Radisson promo code?
Go to radissonhotels.com, search for your hotel and dates, then look for the "Special Rate" or "Promo Code" field on the search results or booking page. Enter your code and search again - rates with the code applied will appear in results. Make sure you're logged in to your Radisson Rewards account to also see the member rate applied automatically.

Can I book Radisson hotels through Agoda or Trip.com and still earn Radisson Rewards points?
Generally no - booking through OTAs at non-member rates typically excludes Radisson Rewards point earning. Some OTA bookings at the Best Available Rate may qualify for a reduced earn rate, but the full 20 points per US$1 only applies to direct bookings. Promo codes like SUMMER26 are only available on the Radisson website, not OTAs. For any stay where points and code discounts matter, book direct.

What is the best Radisson promo code for Asia Pacific?
For Asia Pacific properties in May 2026, SUMMER26 (up to 35% off with breakfast, book by May 28) is the headline deal. The prepaid rate plus Radisson Rewards member rate stack (up to 25% total) is the best ongoing discount for flexible travelers. Log in to Radisson Rewards and select the prepaid option at checkout to see both discounts applied simultaneously.

Does Radisson have a price match guarantee?
Yes - if you find a lower rate online for the same hotel, dates, and room type, Radisson matches it and gives an extra 25% off. Submit the claim within 24 hours of booking and at least 48 hours before arrival. This applies to rates found on OTAs or hotel brand sites. The extra 25% on top of the matched rate makes it worth checking competitors after booking direct.

Is the Radisson prepaid rate refundable?
No - prepaid rates are non-refundable, non-cancellable, and non-adjustable. In Asia Pacific, booking must be made at least 3 days before arrival. Only book prepaid when your travel dates are confirmed. For flexible plans, the Best Available Rate or semi-flexible options are preferable even at a higher starting price.

How many Radisson Rewards points do I need for a free night?
Free night redemption rates vary by property and season. As a guide, budget properties in Asia may require 9,000-15,000 points per night while premium Radisson Blu or Collection properties can require 40,000-70,000 points. Check the Radisson Rewards catalogue for your specific target property. Earning 20 points per US$1 on direct bookings, a US$150/night stay earns 3,000 points - approximately 5-6 stays at that spend level for a budget free night with a Radisson promo code stack.
