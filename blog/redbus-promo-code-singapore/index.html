How to Apply a Redbus Promo Code - Step by Step

  1. Go to redbus.sg and enter your departure city, destination, and travel date.
  2. Browse available buses. Look for redDeals tags on highlighted listings - these have operator discounts pre-applied.
  3. Select your preferred bus, choose your seat, and click "Proceed to Book".
  4. Fill in passenger details and proceed to the payment page.
  5. On the payment page, find the "Enter Offer Code" field. Paste your Redbus promo code and the discount applies immediately.
  6. Complete payment. Wallet cashback (if applicable) credits to your account within 1 hour of booking confirmation.

Popular Routes on Redbus Singapore

Redbus SG covers the core Singapore-Malaysia cross-border routes and select domestic MY routes:

  • Singapore to KL (100+ daily buses): The highest-frequency route on Redbus SG. Operators include KKKL Express, Transtar Travel, Firstcoach, Eltabina Jaya Express, Star Qistina, and StarMart Express. Fares from SGD $25.
  • Singapore to Johor Bahru: Short hop across the Causeway. Multiple departure times throughout the day. Good for day trips to JB for shopping, food, or dental visits.
  • Singapore to Malacca: Popular weekend destination. Direct buses from Golden Mile area, journey around 3.5-4 hours.
  • KL to Penang: Major domestic MY route on Redbus. Comparison between Redbus and BusOnlineTicket pricing is worth doing for this route as competition is strong.
  • KL to Johor Bahru: North-South corridor. Multiple operator options on Redbus MY.
  • Singapore to Genting Highlands: Direct buses available from Golden Mile Complex area, journey approximately 5-6 hours. Popular for weekend casino and theme park trips. Prices from SGD $22-35 depending on operator and seat type.
  • Singapore to Cameron Highlands: Scenic highland destination accessible via bus to Ipoh or direct services. Journey takes 7-8 hours. Book ahead for holiday weekends as this route fills up quickly during school breaks and long weekends.

Redbus App Features

The Redbus app has features that go beyond the website:

  • Live bus tracking: Track your bus location in real time on the day of travel. Useful for knowing exactly when to arrive at the pick-up point.
  • M-Tickets: Digital tickets on your phone - no printing required. QR code scanned by the bus operator at boarding.
  • Seat selection: Visual seat map for selecting your preferred position on the coach. Premium seats (front, lower deck of double-decker) often the same price as standard seats.
  • Wallet management: View and redeem your cashback wallet balance directly in the app.
  • Push notifications for flash sales: The primary way to catch 70% off flash codes before they expire.

Frequently Asked Questions - Redbus Promo Code Singapore

Does SGNEW work for existing Redbus users?
No - SGNEW is a new user code for redbus.sg only. If you already have a Redbus account (including an India or Malaysia account), it may not apply. For existing users, check the redDeals section in search results for operator-specific discounts, and look in the Offers section of the app for current codes available to your account.

How do I apply a Redbus promo code?
Search for your route, select your bus and seat, and proceed to the payment page. On the payment page, find the "Enter Offer Code" field and paste your code. The discount applies immediately. If you have wallet cashback balance, it can be applied on the same page - promo code first, then wallet deduction.

Can I cancel or reschedule a Redbus booking?
Cancellation and rescheduling policies depend on the operator and fare type. Some fares allow free cancellation up to 24 hours before departure, others are non-refundable. Rescheduling is available for eligible bookings via the My Bookings section in the app or website. Redbus charges a rescheduling fee on some operators - check the fare terms at booking.

How does the Redbus wallet work?
The Redbus wallet accumulates cashback from eligible bookings. Once credited (typically within 1 hour of confirmation), the balance appears in your account. At your next booking's payment page, you can apply your wallet balance as a deduction against the fare. Combine a promo code with wallet balance for a stacked discount on a single booking.

Does Redbus have a mobile app for Singapore?
Yes - the redBus app is available on iOS and Android and works for Singapore users booking on redbus.sg. The app adds live bus tracking, m-Tickets, push notifications for flash sale codes, and a visual seat selector. App-exclusive promo codes sometimes appear in the Offers section that aren't available on the website.

Is Redbus cheaper than BusOnlineTicket?
Not consistently - prices for the same operator and departure time are often similar across both platforms. The difference comes from promotional codes: SGNEW (up to 50% off) on Redbus vs the 25% + 25% cashback new user deal on BusOnlineTicket. For first-time users, whichever platform you haven't used yet offers the better new user deal. For ongoing bookings, compare prices per trip and factor in loyalty program value - BOT Miles (BusOnlineTicket) vs wallet cashback (Redbus).

What is the best Redbus promo code for Singapore right now?
For new users, SGNEW gives up to 50% off your first booking on redbus.sg - the best single-use Redbus promo code available in May 2026. For existing users, check the redDeals tab in search results for operator-specific discounts of up to 25%, and enable app push notifications for flash sale codes that can reach 70% off during public holidays and double-digit sale events.
