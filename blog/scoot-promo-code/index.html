Scoot DBS Promo Code - What You Need to Know

The DBS20 Scoot promo code is one of the most consistent deals available, reappearing multiple times per year in partnership with DBS/POSB. Here is how it works:

  • Who qualifies: DBS and POSB credit and debit cardholders. Not available to KrisFlyerUOB bookings.
  • How to use: Visit flyscoot.com/promotions/dbs, log in to your KrisFlyer account, then enter the code during flight search. Look for the "Promo Applied" label before paying.
  • What is discounted: The non-promotional base fare only. Airport taxes, carrier surcharges, baggage, meals, and seat selection are excluded.
  • Travel window: Typically covers travel from 2 to 6 months after the booking window. Blackout periods apply around school holidays.
  • Destinations: 45 to 55 destinations including Japan (Osaka, Tokyo), Korea (Seoul, Jeju), Australia (Sydney, Melbourne, Perth), Thailand, Malaysia, Indonesia, Taiwan, and more.

How to Find and Apply a Scoot Promo Code

  1. Check flyscoot.com/promotions for any current bank or seasonal deals
  2. Subscribe to Scoot email alerts or follow their Telegram for secret codes pushed to subscribers first
  3. For bank codes like DBS20: go to the bank's dedicated Scoot page, log in to KrisFlyer, then enter the code during flight search
  4. Look for the "Promo Applied" confirmation before proceeding to payment

Best Ways to Save on Scoot Flights in 2026

Beyond a one-off Scoot promo code, these strategies give consistent savings:

  • Gotta Scoot Tuesdays: Every Tuesday 12PM to 8PM, Scoot drops discounted fares sitewide. No code needed - just visit flyscoot.com during the window and filter by the cheapest available dates.

  • ScootBiz upgrades on sale: During Gotta Scoot Tuesday sales, ScootBiz fares occasionally appear at close to Economy prices. Worth checking for long-haul routes to Australia.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best Scoot promo code right now?

As of April 2026, DBS/POSB cardholders should check flyscoot.com/promotions/dbs for the latest DBS20 window. The code gives 20% off the base fare on 45 to 55 destinations. For non-DBS users, Gotta Scoot Tuesdays (every Tuesday 12PM to 8PM) is the most reliable weekly source of discounted fares with no code required.

Can I use a Scoot promo code on the app?

Yes. Bank codes like DBS20 can be applied on the Scoot mobile app after logging in to your KrisFlyer account. The app also has exclusive codes not available on desktop - check the Promotions tab and enable push notifications to receive secret codes as soon as they are released.

Does the Scoot DBS promo code apply to the full ticket price?

No. The 20% discount applies to the non-promotional base fare only. Airport taxes, government levies, carrier surcharges, and all add-ons (baggage, meals, seat selection) are excluded. For a SGD 80 base fare, you save SGD 16 - not 20% of the total ticket amount shown at checkout.

Can I earn KrisFlyer miles on discounted Scoot fares?

Yes. Scoot earns 1 KrisFlyer mile per SGD 1 spent on base fares and most add-ons, even on promotional fares. Taxes and fees are excluded. Log in to your KrisFlyer account before booking to ensure miles are credited correctly.

Why is my Scoot promo code not working?

Common reasons: the booking window for that promotion has closed, you are not logged in to a KrisFlyer account, the route or travel date is blacked out, or the fare you selected is already a promotional fare (to which bank codes do not apply). Try a different travel date or check the promotion's destination and travel period list.

Are there Scoot promo codes for ScootBiz?

Bank codes like DBS20 apply to Economy class base fares on selected Scoot-operated flights. ScootBiz upgrades are occasionally discounted during Gotta Scoot Tuesday flash sales - these appear without a code on flyscoot.com during the Tuesday promotion window.
