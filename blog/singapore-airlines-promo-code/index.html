How to Apply a Singapore Airlines Promo Code

  1. Log in to your KrisFlyer account on singaporeair.com before searching for flights
  2. Search for your roundtrip route departing from Singapore
  3. Select your preferred fare class and proceed to checkout
  4. On the payment page, find the "Apply promo code" field and enter your code
  5. Click "Apply" - look for the confirmation that the discount has been applied before paying
  6. Complete payment with the qualifying bank card where required (DBS, Mastercard promos)

Note: most SIA promotion codes apply to roundtrip flights departing Singapore only. One-way bookings and flights departing from other cities do not qualify.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best Singapore Airlines promo code right now?

As of April 2026, the DBS50 booking window runs 14 to 20 April for DBS and POSB cardholders - SGD 50 off roundtrip flights to Jan 2027. The Mastercard MC50OFF code ran until 13 April. For non-bank deals, the KFEARLYBIRD2026 SIA promo applies to KrisFlyer members booking early bird fares on singaporeair.com until November 2026.

Why do Singapore Airlines promo codes sell out so fast?

Bank partnership codes like DBS50 and MC50OFF are issued in limited quantities across all eligible cardholders in Singapore. Thousands of travellers attempt to use them simultaneously when the booking window opens, exhausting the pool within hours. The codes are valid until the quantity runs out, not until the booking window closes.

Do Singapore Airlines promo codes work on all cabin classes?

Most promo codes apply across Economy, Premium Economy, and Business Class on selected fares. First Class is usually excluded. The KFEARLYBIRD2026 student discount applies to Economy and selected upper cabin fares. Always check the specific T&Cs of each code for cabin class restrictions.

Can I earn KrisFlyer miles on discounted fares?

Yes. KrisFlyer miles accrue based on the fare class and route, regardless of whether a promo code was applied. Using a bank card with a high SQ earn rate (such as HSBC TravelOne at 4 mpd or KrisFlyer UOB at 3 mpd) on top of a promo code gives you both an instant discount and ongoing miles accumulation.

Are Singapore Airlines promo fares different from promo codes?

Yes. Promo fares are discounted base fares that appear automatically on singaporeair.com during a booking window - no code needed. Singapore Airlines discounts via codes are separate (usually from bank partnerships) that stack on top of whatever fare is selected. During promo fare windows, combining a low base fare with a bank promo code gives the maximum saving.

Does Singapore Airlines have a promo code for students?

Yes. Singapore Airlines offers up to 10% off selected fares for verified students, plus additional checked baggage allowance. Students need to verify their status through the KrisFlyer programme. The student discount is available on Economy, Premium Economy, and Business Class on selected routes.
