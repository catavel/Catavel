Traveloka PayLater - Buy Now, Pay Later for Travel

Traveloka's PayLater feature lets you confirm bookings now and pay in installments - useful for locking in a sale price before the campaign ends. Key facts:

  • Currently available primarily for Indonesian users (TPayLater). Malaysian and SG users use credit card installment plans offered through bank partnerships instead.
  • Monthly interest rates for TPayLater range from 2.5% to 4.8% - worth factoring into the total cost vs paying upfront.
  • For MY users, CIMB and Maybank offer 0% installment plans on Traveloka bookings through their respective credit card programs - effectively zero-interest PayLater without the TPayLater app feature.
  • Locking in EPIC Sale prices on installment can be worthwhile when hotel rates are 40-60% below normal - the installment interest is often less than what prices will rise to after the sale.

Frequently Asked Questions - Traveloka Promo Code

Where do I enter a Traveloka promo code?
At the payment step of checkout, look for the "Promo Code" or "Coupon Code" field. Enter your code before completing payment. On the app, codes sometimes appear as pre-loaded vouchers in the "Deals" section - tap to apply rather than typing manually.

Can I use a Traveloka promo code on flights and hotels?
It depends on the code. Some are flight-specific (SQSALE, Malaysia Airlines deal), some are hotel-specific (multi-night 5-8% deal, Korea hotels RM200 off), and some are sitewide (new user codes). Always check the terms on the Deals page before applying.

Is the Traveloka EPIC Sale still running in May 2026?
Yes - the EPIC Sale runs through April and May 2026, making this an active window for the deepest Traveloka discounts of the year. Check the Deals section on the app daily as EPIC Hour flash sales (the steepest discounts) appear for 1-2 hour windows only.

Does Traveloka have a rewards program?
Traveloka Points is available in some markets. Members earn points on completed bookings redeemable for discounts on future travel. The program structure varies by country. For SG users, HSBC TravelOne cardholders earn 5 HSBC points per SGD 1 spent on Traveloka - an effective way to earn travel rewards on top of booking discounts.

Can I get a student discount on Traveloka?
Yes in some markets. Traveloka offers special student tickets with up to 20% off fares, along with additional baggage allowances on select airlines. Look for the "Special Tickets" section in the flights search results - eligible student fares are labeled separately from standard fares.

How does the Traveloka referral program work?
Generate your referral link via the Traveloka app and share with friends. When they complete their first booking, they get up to 8% off and you earn savings credits. You can earn up to S$240 in credits through referrals, which apply to your own future bookings. Credits expire within a set period so use them promptly.

Is Traveloka better than Expedia for SEA hotel bookings?
For domestic SEA hotels (staying within Malaysia, Thailand, Philippines), Traveloka usually has more local inventory and sharper prices, especially during EPIC Sale periods. For international hotel chains (Marriott, Hilton in Europe, etc.), Expedia's bank card deals in SG and MY can be more competitive. Compare both platforms for any booking above RM500 before confirming your Traveloka promo code choice.
