Malaysian Bank Card Deals on Traveloka

Traveloka's bank partnerships in Malaysia are among the most active of any OTA in the market. Key banks to check before any larger booking:

  • CIMB: Regular Traveloka campaigns through the year. CIMB cardholders often get additional percentage off on hotel and flight bookings. Check CIMB's travel promotions page and the Traveloka Deals section simultaneously - the rate varies by campaign window.
  • Maybank: Periodic Traveloka-Maybank campaigns, particularly around Payday Sales and double-digit events. Check Maybank2u travel promotions before your next big booking.
  • HSBC TravelOne: Earns 5 HSBC points per RM1 on Traveloka bookings, 8 points per RM1 when processed as overseas spend. Flexible redemption for flights and accommodation. Effective stacking tool for frequent Traveloka users.
  • RHB and AmBank: Occasional Traveloka campaigns. Monitor RHB's deals page during major Traveloka sale periods (EPIC, 12.12) for card-specific offers.
  • Hainan Airlines: 4% off with min spend RM1,500 - niche but worth knowing for KL-China routes.

Domestic Destinations - Best Traveloka Hotel Deals in Malaysia

Traveloka has strong domestic inventory for Malaysian travelers. Here are the destinations where Traveloka pricing consistently competes well:

  • Langkawi: Resort hotels and chalets at competitive MYR rates. The 3-night 8% hotel deal is worth stacking on Langkawi resort bookings above RM500/night.
  • Penang: Georgetown heritage hotels and Batu Ferringhi beach resorts. Traveloka often has local guesthouses not listed on international platforms.
  • Cameron Highlands: Tea estate stays and hill resort packages. Domestic roundtrip flight deals (KL-Ipoh + car) sometimes bundle on Traveloka.
  • Sabah and Sarawak: Kota Kinabalu beach resorts, Kuching boutique hotels. Traveloka's domestic flight coverage for Malaysia East is strong.
  • Johor Bahru: Hotels near Legoland and theme parks, good for families crossing from Singapore.

Malaysian Public Holiday Booking Tips

Hotel and flight prices on Traveloka spike predictably around Malaysian public holidays. Book in advance for better rates:

  • Hari Raya Aidilfitri: Book domestic flights and hometown-route hotels 6-8 weeks ahead. Prices double in the 2-week window before the holiday.
  • Chinese New Year: Popular East Malaysia and Thailand routes fill fast. January bookings for CNY travel are standard for budget-conscious travelers.
  • Merdeka (Aug 31) and Malaysia Day (Sep 16): Long weekend trips to Langkawi and Penang are popular. Book 3-4 weeks ahead for any resort that sleeps more than 50 guests.
  • School holidays (Nov-Dec): Domestic packages peak. Counter-strategy: book international destinations (Korea, Japan, Thailand) during local school holidays when SEA domestic demand is high but international competition is lower.

Frequently Asked Questions - Traveloka Promo Code Malaysia

What is the best Traveloka promo code Malaysia right now?
For new users, the app 8% off (up to RM100) is the strongest single code available. For existing users, SQSALE on Fridays gives up to 50% off Singapore Airlines flights to Japan, Korea, and Hong Kong with no minimum spend. For hotel bookings, the 3+ night 8% deal valid until May 31 stacks well with bank card discounts.

Does Traveloka Malaysia have a PayLater option?
The full TPayLater installment product is currently available to Indonesian users only. Malaysian users can access 0% installment plans through CIMB and Maybank credit cards when booking on Traveloka - effectively the same outcome. Check your bank's installment eligibility for travel bookings before confirming.

How do I apply the SQSALE code on Traveloka?
SQSALE is an app-only code, available every Friday starting at 9AM Malaysia time. Open the Traveloka app, search for Singapore Airlines flights to Japan, China, Korea, HK, or Taiwan departing from Malaysia, and apply SQSALE at checkout. The 50% discount (up to RM250) applies to the base fare. Availability is limited - the best seats at the deepest discount go within the first hour.

Can I combine a Traveloka promo code with a bank card deal?
Yes in many cases. The promo code applies first, then your bank card deal (if applicable) applies at payment. For example: hotel 8% multi-night code applied at checkout + CIMB card additional discount at payment. Always verify both are eligible for the same booking type before confirming.

What is the Traveloka EPIC Sale and is it still active?
The EPIC Sale is Traveloka's biggest annual campaign, running April through May with up to 60% off flights, hotels, and activities. As of May 2026 it is still active. EPIC Hour flash deals - the deepest discounts of the entire year - run for 1-2 hours within the EPIC Sale window. Check the Traveloka app daily during this period and enable push notifications to catch them.

Are Traveloka prices in MYR for Malaysian users?
Yes - traveloka.com/en-my displays prices in MYR by default. International hotel bookings may show original currency with MYR equivalent. When using promo codes, the discount is calculated and applied in MYR at checkout so you see the final amount before payment.

How do I find the best Traveloka promo code Malaysia deals without missing flash sales?
Three steps: (1) Download the Traveloka app and enable push notifications for deal alerts. (2) Check the Deals section in-app daily, especially on Fridays for SQSALE and at month-end for Payday Sales. (3) Follow Traveloka Malaysia on social media - flash sale codes are often posted 30-60 minutes before they go live on the platform.
