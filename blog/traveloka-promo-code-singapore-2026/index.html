How to Apply a Traveloka Promo Code Singapore

Redeeming your Traveloka discount code is straightforward. Follow these steps:

  1. Go to Traveloka and search for your flight, hotel, or activity.
  2. Select your preferred option and proceed to the checkout page.
  3. Look for the "Promo Code" or "Voucher Code" field on the payment page.
  4. Enter your code exactly as shown above (codes are case-sensitive).
  5. Click "Apply" - the discount will reflect in your total before payment.
  6. Complete your booking using the required card if the promo is card-linked.

Note: Traveloka promo codes are typically applied during checkout, not before. Make sure you're logged in to your Traveloka account before applying any code.

Do I need a specific bank card to use Traveloka promo codes?
Yes, all the codes listed above are card-linked. CITIXPLORES requires a Citi card, DBSSPECIAL requires a DBS or POSB card, and the MAYBANKWONDERS and MAYBANKTRAVEL codes require a Maybank Singapore card. You must pay with the required card for the discount to apply.

Can I combine multiple Traveloka promo codes in one booking?
Generally, Traveloka allows only one promo code per transaction. You cannot stack two discount codes. However, some in-app promotions or cashback from your bank may apply on top of the promo code discount - check with your bank for details.
