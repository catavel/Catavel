Trip.com Rewards - How Trip Coins Work

Trip Coins is Trip.com's free rewards program. Every eligible booking earns coins redeemable as cash on future bookings:

  • Earn Trip Coins on flights, hotels, car rentals, trains, attractions, and airport transfers
  • 100 Trip Coins is worth approximately S$0.73 / HK$4 / RM3.30 depending on market
  • Coins can be used like cash for prepaid bookings across all product categories
  • Membership tiers (Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond) unlock higher earn rates and exclusive member-day deals
  • Writing verified reviews earns bonus coins - worth doing after each trip to build your balance
  • Trip.com Member Day campaigns (run periodically) offer the steepest hotel discounts exclusively for logged-in members

Frequently Asked Questions - Trip.com Promo Code

Where do I enter a Trip.com promo code?
On the app: go to Account, tap "Promo Code" at the top right, and add your code before searching - it auto-applies to eligible bookings at checkout. On desktop: click the account icon, select "Promo Codes" from the dropdown, and enter manually. The app route is recommended as most high-value codes are app-only.

Can I use more than one Trip.com promo code per booking?
Generally no - Trip.com allows one promo code per booking. The exception is some cruise deals where a standard coupon can stack with campaign-specific offers. Bank card deals (Visa 8% hotels, Citi S$100) are separate from promo codes and can often be combined - the promo code reduces the price first, then your card discount applies at payment.

Why isn't my Trip.com promo code working?
Most common causes: the code is app-only and you're on the website; your account region doesn't match the code's target market; the booking category doesn't match (hotel code used for flights); minimum spend not met; or the code has expired. Switch to the app and verify your account region setting matches the deal's market before retrying.

What is WOW Wednesday on Trip.com?
WOW Wednesday is Trip.com's weekly flash sale with up to 50% off selected hotels and flights. Deals are typically live for 24-48 hours and stock is limited. For SG users, the Thursday 12PM deal schedule is confirmed in their promotions calendar. Enable app push notifications to catch deals as soon as they go live.

How do Trip Coins work and are they worth using?
Every eligible Trip.com booking earns Trip Coins, with 100 coins worth roughly S$0.73. They accumulate across all product categories and can be redeemed like cash on future prepaid bookings. For travelers who book hotels, flights, and activities regularly across platforms, using Trip.com consistently for qualifying bookings builds a meaningful balance over 3-6 months. Writing post-trip reviews adds bonus coins on top.

Does Trip.com have a price guarantee?
Yes - Trip.com Singapore offers a Price Guarantee that locks your flight fare once payment is confirmed, protecting against post-booking price increases. This applies to the fare you paid, not as a price-match against other platforms. Always compare prices before booking rather than relying on the guarantee as a post-purchase safety net.

Is the Visa 3%/8% deal on Trip.com available to all cardholders?
Yes - the Visa deal (3% off flights, 8% off hotels) applies to any Visa credit or debit card regardless of issuing bank. This is Trip.com's broadest ongoing trip.com promo code equivalent - no code entry required, just pay with any Visa card. Valid across all markets until January 2027.
