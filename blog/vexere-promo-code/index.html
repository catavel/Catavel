What is Vexere? - Vietnam's Transport Booking Hub

Vexere is the market-leader for transport booking in Vietnam, covering buses, trains, flights, and car rentals on a single platform:

  • Official Vietnam Railways partner: Real-time seat maps, instant e-tickets, and all DSVN discounts automatically applied. No queuing at the station.
  • Scale: 5,000+ routes, 2,000+ bus operators, 5,000+ ticketing agents across Vietnam
  • Foreign traveler friendly: Full English interface, international Visa/Mastercard payment, dedicated English customer support
  • E-tickets on all routes: Show QR code on phone - no physical ticket printing required
  • Transparent pricing: Same price as station or cheaper (exclusive discount codes layer on top of base fares)
  • Bus types available: Standard seater, limousine 9-11 seat, sleeper bus (41-seat), cabin sleeper (luxury 20-berth), and VIP coaches

How to Book on Vexere as a Foreign Traveler

  1. Go to vexere.com (English version) or download the Vexere app. The search interface is available in English.
  2. Enter your departure city, destination, and travel date. Results show all operators, departure times, bus types, and prices.
  3. Filter by bus type (sleeper, limousine, cabin) and check customer ratings per operator before selecting.
  4. Select your seat on the visual seat map - choose lower deck for sleeper buses (smoother ride).
  5. At checkout, select your payment method. International Visa and Mastercard work reliably. Vietnamese e-wallets (VNPAY, ShopeePay, ZaloPay, MoMo) give extra discounts if you have access.
  6. Enter any applicable discount code in the coupon field before paying.
  7. Receive e-ticket by email and SMS. Show the QR code to the operator at boarding.

Vietnam Train Booking - Reunification Express Guide

Vexere is an official partner of Vietnam Railways, making it the most reliable way to book the Reunification Express (tàu Bắc-Nam) online as a foreigner:

  • Updated 2026 timetable: New schedule applied from March 9, 2026, with further updates from May 15, 2026. Trains SE1/SE2 through SE11/SE12 run daily on the full Hanoi-HCMC route (1,726km, 30-35 hours).
  • Scenic segment: The Da Nang to Hue section via Hai Van Pass is one of the most scenic train rides in Southeast Asia - cliff-edge coastal views of the East Sea. Book the 2-4 hour segment separately for a day experience.
  • Seat classes: Hard seat (cheapest), soft seat, hard sleeper 6-berth, soft sleeper 4-berth (most popular for foreigners), VIP 2-berth. Book a soft sleeper lower berth for the best overnight experience.
  • Hanoi to Sapa train: The overnight Hanoi to Lao Cai route (then bus to Sapa) is bookable via Vexere. Journey is approximately 8-9 hours overnight, arriving Lao Cai station early morning.
  • Advance booking: Book 30-60 days ahead for the best prices and seat selection. Discounts of 5-15% apply for bookings 10-40 days before departure on long-haul routes.

Bus Types on Vexere - Which to Choose

  • Limousine (9-11 seats): Best for comfort and speed. Fewer passengers, more legroom, usually direct routes with fewer stops. Good for routes under 5 hours.
  • Sleeper bus (41 seats): Budget-friendly overnight option. Reclining flat berths on two decks. Lower berths are smoother (less movement on winding roads). Book lower deck when available.
  • Cabin sleeper (20 berths): Luxury version of sleeper bus. Private enclosed cabin with door, more space, AC, better privacy. Best for overnight routes like HCMC-Nha Trang.
  • Standard seater (45 seats): Cheapest option. Acceptable for short to medium routes (under 4 hours). Fine for Hue-Da Nang and similar legs.
Catavel Tip: For overnight buses (HCMC to Nha Trang, Da Lat, or Hanoi to Sapa), the cabin sleeper saves you a hotel night. A cabin berth at VND 350,000-500,000 is typically cheaper than a budget guesthouse, and you arrive at the destination ready to start the day. Book the cabin option even if it costs VND 50,000-100,000 more than a standard sleeper.

Frequently Asked Questions - Vexere Promo Code

Is Vexere safe for foreign travelers to use?
Yes. Vexere is Vietnam's largest transport booking platform and an official strategic partner of Vietnam Railways (DSVN). It accepts international Visa and Mastercard without the payment gateway issues common on DSVN's own website. E-tickets are issued instantly and the QR code is all you need at boarding. English customer support is available via email at lienhe@vexere.com and via in-app chat.

Can I use a Vexere promo code with an international credit card?
Yes for most codes. The new user discount, round-trip discount, and flash sale codes apply regardless of payment method. The VNPAY-QR and e-wallet deals (ShopeePay, ZaloPay, MoMo) require Vietnamese payment apps. For foreign travelers without these wallets, stick to the early booking discount (5-15% for booking 10-40 days ahead) and any active promo code at checkout - both work with international cards.

Is Vexere cheaper than booking at the bus station directly?
Often yes, never more expensive. Vexere prices match the operator's own pricing and frequently undercut station walk-in rates through exclusive discount codes. More importantly, Vexere lets you compare all operators on a route simultaneously and book from anywhere - avoiding last-minute station scrambles, especially during Tet and public holiday peak periods when buses sell out.

What payment methods work on Vexere for tourists?
International Visa and Mastercard work reliably. Vietnamese e-wallets (VNPAY-QR, ShopeePay, ZaloPay, MoMo) give extra discounts if you have access. The Vexere app also supports local banking apps via QR payment. For most foreign travelers, international credit card is the simplest option - just be aware that some banks may flag Vietnam transactions as foreign spend.

Can I cancel a Vexere booking if my plans change?
Yes for many bookings. Vexere supports online cancellation and refund requests via the app or website, without needing to visit the station physically. Refund eligibility and timelines vary by operator and fare type - check the cancellation policy on each listing before booking. Train tickets booked via Vexere can be cancelled or changed via email request, compared to the physical counter requirement for direct DSVN bookings.

How far in advance should I book Vietnam bus or train tickets?
For trains: 30-60 days ahead for the best prices and seat class availability, especially soft sleeper 4-berth. For the Hanoi-HCMC Reunification Express during Tet (Vietnamese New Year), book 2-3 months ahead. For buses: 1-7 days is usually sufficient on most routes, except during Tet and major holidays when popular sleeper routes sell out 2-3 weeks ahead.

Does Vexere cover all Vietnam destinations or just major cities?
Vexere covers 160+ train stations and 5,000+ bus routes reaching every province in Vietnam. Popular tourist destinations (Sapa, Ha Long Bay, Hoi An, Da Lat, Nha Trang, Phu Quoc, Mui Ne) all have multiple operator options. Even less-visited areas like Ha Giang, Pu Luong, and Phong Nha are bookable via Vexere's extensive bus operator network - making it the most comprehensive transport platform for any Vexere promo code search across Vietnam.
