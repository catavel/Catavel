What is Wing On Travel? - 60 Years in Hong Kong

Wing On Travel (永安旅遊) was established in Hong Kong in 1964, making it one of the city's oldest and most recognized travel brands. Key facts:

  • Part of the Wing On Group, a major HK conglomerate with businesses spanning retail, property, and financial services
  • Books flights from 50+ airlines serving 200+ destinations worldwide
  • 1 million+ hotel options available via the platform
  • Full-service: flights, hotels, self-guided packages (自由行), guided tours, Macau ferry tickets, and travel insurance
  • HK-centric: deep inventory on HK-Macau, HK-China routes, and Greater Bay Area travel
  • Available via website (wingontravel.com) and mobile app (iOS + Android, primarily Chinese interface)
