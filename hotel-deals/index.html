    Hotel Deals Asia - Frequently Asked Questions

    How does Catavel verify these hotel deals are real discounts?

    Every hotel deal listed here is sourced directly from Klook, which publishes both the original price and the discounted rate on each listing. Catavel pulls this data weekly, checks that the discount percentage is calculated correctly (original minus deal price divided by original), and only displays deals where the saving is 10% or more. Deals that expire or drop below our quality threshold are removed from the feed automatically. What you see here reflects active, bookable offers - not evergreen placeholder prices.

    Which countries have the best hotel deals on Klook right now?

    Japan and South Korea consistently offer the steepest hotel deals on Klook, partly because of currency exchange rates that favour Singapore and Malaysia travelers and partly because Klook has deep inventory in those markets. Thailand (Bangkok especially) also has strong discounts on midrange and 4-star properties. Hong Kong deals tend to be more competitive on room rate than percentage, while Malaysian domestic hotels often appear with bundle discounts when paired with attraction tickets. Use the Country filter on this page to compare across destinations and sort by discount to see the top hotel deals Asia has to offer right now.

    Are these prices the same as booking directly on Klook?

    Yes - the prices you see on Catavel are pulled from Klook in real time. Clicking Book takes you directly to the Klook listing where the same price is shown. We do not mark up prices or earn anything from the price itself. Catavel earns a small affiliate commission from Klook when you complete a booking, but this comes from Klook's marketing budget and does not affect what you pay. In some cases, using a Klook promo code at checkout can bring the price down further beyond what is shown here.

    How often are hotel deals updated on Catavel?

    The hotel deals feed is updated weekly. New deals are added when Klook launches promotions for upcoming travel periods - typically around school holidays, festive seasons, and major travel months for the Singapore and Malaysia market (June, November, December, and Chinese New Year). Expired deals are removed within 24-48 hours of expiry. The "Last updated" date on each deal card indicates when that specific listing was last verified. If you spot a deal that looks outdated, the Klook listing page will show the correct current price when you click through.

    Can I use Klook promo codes on top of these hotel deal discounts?

    In most cases, yes. Klook promo codes work on the cart total, so if a hotel deal is already discounted 20% and you have a code for an additional 5% off, you can stack both. Some Klook campaigns exclude hotel products or specific properties from code usage - the T&Cs on the promo code will specify. Check our Klook promo codes page for the latest verified codes, including any hotel-specific offers. First-time Klook users in Singapore and Malaysia often qualify for a welcome discount that applies to hotels as well as activities.

    Do I need to sign up to access these hotel deals?

    No account or sign-up is needed to browse hotel deals on Catavel. The entire page loads instantly with no registration wall. When you click Book, you are taken directly to the Klook listing. You will need a Klook account to complete the booking on their side, but creating one is free and takes under a minute. Catavel will never ask for your email, payment information, or personal data. The goal is to get you to the best hotel deals Asia has available as quickly as possible, with zero friction on our end.

    What is the difference between Klook hotel deals and Trip.com or Agoda hotel deals?

    Klook hotel deals are often bundled with experiences - an airport transfer, a breakfast credit, or an attraction ticket included in the room rate. This makes them especially strong value for activity-heavy trips to Japan, South Korea, or Thailand. Trip.com hotel deals tend to be stronger for flight-plus-hotel packages, particularly for travelers departing from Singapore or Kuala Lumpur, and their loyalty programme adds extra value for frequent bookers. Agoda is typically best for last-minute hotel deals in Southeast Asia, with a large budget and boutique inventory in Bangkok, KL, and Bali. The right platform depends on your destination and travel style - Catavel covers all three so you can compare across sources.
